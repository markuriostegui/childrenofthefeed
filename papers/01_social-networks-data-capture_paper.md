The FTC's 2024 report on social media and video-streaming data practices gives this chapter its strongest regulatory anchor. ## Historical or Institutional Context

The FTC's 2024 report on social media and video-streaming data practices gives this chapter its strongest regulatory anchor. The platform model is documented as one of extraction, retention, combination, and monetization, not as a neutral relay of user speech. That record is already enough to weaken the old fiction that sovereign-scale platforms are passive intermediaries. ^[Sources: FTC, [A Look Behind the Screens: Examining...](https://www.ftc.gov/reports/look-behind-screens-examining-data-practices-social-media-video-streaming-services) (n.d.; see [Appendix A1](#appendix-a1-a-look-behind-the-screens-examining-the-data-practices-of-social-media-and-video-streaming-services))]

## Main Analysis

The FTC's 2019 Facebook action matters because Cambridge Analytica was not just a reputational scandal. It became a structural compliance event with board-level oversight duties and a formal federal record of deceptive and insufficiently controlled data governance. ^[Sources: FTC, [FTC Imposes $5 Billion Penalty and Sw...](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2019/07/ftc-imposes-5-billion-penalty-sweeping-new-privacy-restrictions-facebook) (2019-07-24; see [Appendix A2](#appendix-a2-ftc-imposes-5-billion-penalty-and-sweeping-new-privacy-restrictions-on-facebook))]

Facebook's own `S-1` and later Meta proxy filings document Peter Thiel as an early investor and a long-lived governance actor. That does not prove clandestine command. It does prove continuity between platform surveillance, elite venture governance, and later state-adjacent technology networks. ^[Sources: SEC, [Facebook, Inc. Registration Statement...](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000119312512034517/d287954ds1.htm) (2012-02-01; see [Appendix A3](#appendix-a3-facebook-inc-registration-statement-on-form-s-1)); SEC, [Meta Platforms, Inc. 2022 Definitive...](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000132680122000043/meta2022definitiveproxysta.htm) (2022-04-08; see [Appendix A4](#appendix-a4-meta-platforms-inc-2022-definitive-proxy-statement))]

Meta's 2021 rebrand and 2023 `10-K` show that the company attached a metaverse-centered strategic narrative to a still-dominant advertising machine. The rebrand did not function as a data-governance reset. It functioned as narrative redirection while the extraction engine remained intact and financially central. ^[Sources: Meta, [The Facebook Company Is Now Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2021/10/facebook-company-is-now-meta/) (2021-10-28; see [Appendix A5](#appendix-a5-the-facebook-company-is-now-meta)); U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, [Meta Platforms, Inc. 2023 Annual Repo...](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000132680124000012/meta-20231231.htm) (2024-02-02; see [Appendix A6](#appendix-a6-meta-platforms-inc-2023-annual-report-form-10-k))]

The documented platform record therefore shows more than large-scale surveillance. It shows social participation being routed through privately governed interfaces that could rank visibility, shape reach, and condition economic opportunity. Interpretation: this is the earlier precedent for the later AI story. Once an intermediary controls the interface layer, it can influence value without producing the underlying social life itself. ^[Sources: FTC, [A Look Behind the Screens: Examining...](https://www.ftc.gov/reports/look-behind-screens-examining-data-practices-social-media-video-streaming-services) (n.d.; see [Appendix A1](#appendix-a1-a-look-behind-the-screens-examining-the-data-practices-of-social-media-and-video-streaming-services)); FTC, [FTC Imposes $5 Billion Penalty and Sw...](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2019/07/ftc-imposes-5-billion-penalty-sweeping-new-privacy-restrictions-facebook) (2019-07-24; see [Appendix A2](#appendix-a2-ftc-imposes-5-billion-penalty-and-sweeping-new-privacy-restrictions-on-facebook)); U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, [Meta Platforms, Inc. 2023 Annual Repo...](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000132680124000012/meta-20231231.htm) (2024-02-02; see [Appendix A6](#appendix-a6-meta-platforms-inc-2023-annual-report-form-10-k))]

Interpretation: Chapter `01` begins the case for `Refactor Section 230` by showing that reach, design, targeting, monetization, and recommendation are not incidental platform features. They are the product. A sovereign-scale platform framework should therefore track systemic design power rather than cling to passive-hosting logic built for an earlier internet. ^[Sources: FTC, [A Look Behind the Screens: Examining...](https://www.ftc.gov/reports/look-behind-screens-examining-data-practices-social-media-video-streaming-services) (n.d.; see [Appendix A1](#appendix-a1-a-look-behind-the-screens-examining-the-data-practices-of-social-media-and-video-streaming-services)); FTC, [FTC Imposes $5 Billion Penalty and Sw...](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2019/07/ftc-imposes-5-billion-penalty-sweeping-new-privacy-restrictions-facebook) (2019-07-24; see [Appendix A2](#appendix-a2-ftc-imposes-5-billion-penalty-and-sweeping-new-privacy-restrictions-on-facebook)); U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, [Meta Platforms, Inc. 2023 Annual Repo...](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000132680124000012/meta-20231231.htm) (2024-02-02; see [Appendix A6](#appendix-a6-meta-platforms-inc-2023-annual-report-form-10-k))]

## Reform Relevance

Once social life is reorganized around measurable engagement, extractive targeting, and recommendation systems, platforms stop looking like passive infrastructure and start looking like privately governed civic systems. That is why the dossier's Section `230` argument begins here: sovereign-scale platforms should be judged by the harms and dependencies created by their design, amplification, and monetization architecture. ^[Sources: FTC, [A Look Behind the Screens: Examining...](https://www.ftc.gov/reports/look-behind-screens-examining-data-practices-social-media-video-streaming-services) (n.d.; see [Appendix A1](#appendix-a1-a-look-behind-the-screens-examining-the-data-practices-of-social-media-and-video-streaming-services)); FTC, [FTC Imposes $5 Billion Penalty and Sw...](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2019/07/ftc-imposes-5-billion-penalty-sweeping-new-privacy-restrictions-facebook) (2019-07-24; see [Appendix A2](#appendix-a2-ftc-imposes-5-billion-penalty-and-sweeping-new-privacy-restrictions-on-facebook))]

## Conclusion

Once social life is reorganized around measurable engagement, extractive targeting, and recommendation systems, platforms stop looking like passive infrastructure and start looking like privately governed civic systems. The next problem is not only that platforms captured behavior.
