The moral-authority layer widened too. Dario Amodei publicly warned that AI-driven wealth concentration could destabilize democracy while also describing the founders' 80% wealth pledge. They show how moral positioning becomes part of the legitimacy field itself. ^[Sources: Dario Amodei, [The Adolescence of Technology](https://darioamodei.com/essay/the-adolescence-of-technology) (2026-01-01; see [Appendix A12](#appendix-a12-the-adolescence-of-technology)); The Holy See, [Encyclical Letter of His Holiness Leo...](https://www.vatican.va/content/leo-xiv/en/encyclicals/documents/20260515-magnifica-humanitas.html) (2026-05-15; see [Appendix A13](#appendix-a13-encyclical-letter-of-his-holiness-leo-xiv-magnifica-humanitas))]

Another part of the legitimacy problem is private survivability hedging. In 2016, Sam Altman publicly described storing guns, gold, potassium iodide, antibiotics, gas masks, and having a New Zealand fallback plan tied to Peter Thiel. None of this proves that these actors expect imminent collapse or are engineering it. But it does sharpen a bounded political question: legitimacy weakens when elites ask publics and states to normalize dependency on systems they govern while privately investing in exemption from shared risk. ^[Sources: Tad Friend, [Sam Altman's Manifest Destiny](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2016/10/10/sam-altmans-manifest-destiny) (2016-10-03; see [Appendix A14](#appendix-a14-sam-altman-s-manifest-destiny)); Makena Kelly, [Inside Mark Zuckerberg’s Top-Secret H...](https://www.wired.com/story/mark-zuckerberg-inside-hawaii-compound) (2023-12-14; see [Appendix A15](#appendix-a15-inside-mark-zuckerbergs-top-secret-hawaii-compound)); Charlotte Phillipp, [Mark Zuckerberg Downplays His 5,000-S...](https://people.com/mark-zuckerberg-hawaii-bunker-5000-square-feet-8765376) (2024-12-20; see [Appendix A16](#appendix-a16-mark-zuckerberg-downplays-his-5-000-square-foot-underground-bunker-in-hawaii-just-a-little-shelter))]
