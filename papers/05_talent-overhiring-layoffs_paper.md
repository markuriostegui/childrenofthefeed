## Historical or Institutional Context

Section `174` is a real structural factor. IRS guidance and congressional repair efforts show that domestic research and software expenses were caught in a more punitive amortization regime after 2021, and Congress itself treated the burden as significant enough to attempt reversal. This is not the whole story, but it is not rhetorical decoration either. ^[Sources: Internal Revenue Service, [Notice 2023-63: Guidance on Section 1...](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/n-23-63.pdf) (2023-09-08; see [Appendix A1](#appendix-a1-notice-2023-63-guidance-on-section-174-and-specified-research-or-experimental-expenditures)); Congress.gov, [H.R. 7024: Tax Relief for American Fa...](https://www.congress.gov/bill/118th-congress/house-bill/7024) (2024-01-16; see [Appendix A5](#appendix-a5-h-r-7024-tax-relief-for-american-families-and-workers-act-of-2024)); Congress.gov / CRS, [H.R. 7024: Tax Relief for American Fa...](https://www.congress.gov/bill/118th-congress/house-bill/7024) (2024-01-31; see [Appendix A6](#appendix-a6-h-r-7024-tax-relief-for-american-families-and-workers-act-of-2024-crs-summary)); Congress.gov / Library of Congress, [H.R. 7024 text: Deduction for domesti...](https://www.congress.gov/bill/118th-congress/house-bill/7024/text) (2024-03-21; see [Appendix A7](#appendix-a7-h-r-7024-text-deduction-for-domestic-research-and-experimental-expenditures))]

## Main Analysis

The post-pandemic overhiring record is also strong. Executive statements and major reporting across Meta, Amazon, and Salesforce show a later turn toward efficiency, focus, and cost discipline after an earlier period of aggressive expansion. ^[Sources: Meta, [Mark Zuckerberg's Message to Meta Emp...](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/03/mark-zuckerberg-meta-year-of-efficiency/) (2023-03-14; see [Appendix A3](#appendix-a3-mark-zuckerberg-s-message-to-meta-employees)); Associated Press, [Meta posts lower Q4 profit, announces...](https://apnews.com/article/b884c9ac96be1c22f81d7243176a321d) (2023-02-01; see [Appendix A8](#appendix-a8-meta-posts-lower-q4-profit-announces-huge-stock-buyback)); Associated Press, [Meta posts sharp profit, revenue incr...](https://apnews.com/article/abc3e389d97fb97c661ccb6acfcf65c7) (2024-02-01; see [Appendix A9](#appendix-a9-meta-posts-sharp-profit-revenue-increase-in-q4-thanks-to-cost-cuts-and-advertising-rebound)); Associated Press, [Amazon, Salesforce jettison jobs in l...](https://apnews.com/article/bbb2f3a09eafdfc7cda54a3b87e538ad) (2023-01-04; see [Appendix A2](#appendix-a2-amazon-salesforce-jettison-jobs-in-latest-tech-worker-purge))]

Top-tier reporting ties the broader correction to weaker demand, margin pressure, and investor impatience, not only to AI. That matters because it prevents a monocausal story. The financial correction was already underway before AI became the universal explanation for it. ^[Sources: Financial Times, [AI exuberance masks broad weakness in...](https://www.ft.com/content/0b14d871-2d19-46f1-9660-5466301e8066) (2024-09-09; see [Appendix A4](#appendix-a4-ai-exuberance-masks-broad-weakness-in-tech-sector-say-investors)); Axios, [Big Tech layoffs shattered industry,...](https://www.axios.com/2024/01/03/tech-layoffs-google-microsoft-employees-impact-anxiety) (2024-01-03; see [Appendix A10](#appendix-a10-big-tech-layoffs-shattered-industry-worker-confidence))]

The BIS annual report adds the macro-financial layer. By June 2026, a major multilateral institution was openly warning that the AI capex race was outrunning earnings and free cash flow, with leverage and financing structures becoming part of the risk profile. The system was promising abundance while leaning on increasingly fragile financial scaffolding. ^[Sources: Bank for International Settlements, [Annual Economic Report 2026](https://www.bis.org/publ/arpdf/ar2026e.pdf) (2026-06-28; see [Appendix A11](#appendix-a11-annual-economic-report-2026))]

Interpretation: this is where the dossier's economic thesis sharpens. AI did not invent the correction, but it became the language through which it was justified. The same story that encloses captured human production also helps reclassify labor cuts as technological destiny instead of the outcome of financial structure, policy, and managerial excess. That interpretation supports Chapter `04` by showing how enclosure and labor subordination move together. ^[Sources: Meta, [Mark Zuckerberg's Message to Meta Emp...](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/03/mark-zuckerberg-meta-year-of-efficiency/) (2023-03-14; see [Appendix A3](#appendix-a3-mark-zuckerberg-s-message-to-meta-employees)); Financial Times, [AI exuberance masks broad weakness in...](https://www.ft.com/content/0b14d871-2d19-46f1-9660-5466301e8066) (2024-09-09; see [Appendix A4](#appendix-a4-ai-exuberance-masks-broad-weakness-in-tech-sector-say-investors)); Bank for International Settlements, [Annual Economic Report 2026](https://www.bis.org/publ/arpdf/ar2026e.pdf) (2026-06-28; see [Appendix A11](#appendix-a11-annual-economic-report-2026))]

## Reform Relevance

If this reading holds, then the AI boom is also a class story. It narrows the protected tier, weakens the technical middle class, and presents downward pressure on labor as the rational price of the future. That strengthens the patrimony argument: a system built from collective human production is being enclosed while many of the humans who made it possible are told the future no longer needs them on equal terms. ^[Sources: Internal Revenue Service, [Notice 2023-63: Guidance on Section 1...](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/n-23-63.pdf) (2023-09-08; see [Appendix A1](#appendix-a1-notice-2023-63-guidance-on-section-174-and-specified-research-or-experimental-expenditures)); Bank for International Settlements, [Annual Economic Report 2026](https://www.bis.org/publ/arpdf/ar2026e.pdf) (2026-06-28; see [Appendix A11](#appendix-a11-annual-economic-report-2026)); TwinChat, [AI Copyright Weights: A New Frontier...](https://medium.com/twinchat/ai-copyright-weights-a-new-frontier-in-intellectual-property-law-d8ee1b6c55ee) (n.d.; see [Appendix A12](#appendix-a12-ai-copyright-weights-a-new-frontier-in-intellectual-property-law))]

## Conclusion

If this reading holds, then the AI boom is also a class story. That contradiction sharpens in the next chapter. In the architecture of this series, Chapter 05 therefore functions as a self-contained argument while also advancing the cumulative path toward the dossier's three reforms.
