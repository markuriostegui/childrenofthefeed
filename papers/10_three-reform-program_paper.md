### Refactor Section 230

Documented problem: sovereign-scale platforms do not merely host speech. They structure exposure, optimize amplification, monetize targeting, and govern the conditions under which civic, psychological, and political harm scales. ^[Sources: FTC, [A Look Behind the Screens: Examining...](https://www.ftc.gov/reports/look-behind-screens-examining-data-practices-social-media-video-streaming-services) (n.d.; see [Appendix A4](#appendix-a4-a-look-behind-the-screens-examining-the-data-practices-of-social-media-and-video-streaming-services)); HHS, [Social Media and Youth Mental Health:...](https://www.hhs.gov/surgeongeneral/priorities/youth-mental-health/social-media/index.html) (n.d.; see [Appendix A5](#appendix-a5-social-media-and-youth-mental-health-the-u-s-surgeon-general-s-advisory)); arXiv, [When Kids Mode Isn't For Kids: Invest...](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.00299) (2025-06-30; see [Appendix A10](#appendix-a10-when-kids-mode-isn-t-for-kids-investigating-tiktok-s-under-13-experience)); Pew Research Center, [Social Media Seen as Mostly Good for...](https://www.pewresearch.org/global/2022/12/06/social-media-seen-as-mostly-good-for-democracy-across-many-nations-but-u-s-is-a-major-outlier/) (2022-12-06; see [Appendix A11](#appendix-a11-social-media-seen-as-mostly-good-for-democracy-across-many-nations-but-u-s-is-a-major-outlier))]

Interpretation: the legal principle should shift from passive hosting to systemic design responsibility. Refactoring Section `230` does not require treating every user post as if it were authored by the platform. It requires treating sovereign-scale platforms as infrastructures whose reach, design, and monetized amplification create duties commensurate with their power. ^[Sources: FTC, [A Look Behind the Screens: Examining...](https://www.ftc.gov/reports/look-behind-screens-examining-data-practices-social-media-video-streaming-services) (n.d.; see [Appendix A4](#appendix-a4-a-look-behind-the-screens-examining-the-data-practices-of-social-media-and-video-streaming-services)); HHS, [Social Media and Youth Mental Health:...](https://www.hhs.gov/surgeongeneral/priorities/youth-mental-health/social-media/index.html) (n.d.; see [Appendix A5](#appendix-a5-social-media-and-youth-mental-health-the-u-s-surgeon-general-s-advisory)); arXiv, [Quantifying social organization and p...](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.00590) (2020-10-01; see [Appendix A12](#appendix-a12-quantifying-social-organization-and-political-polarization-in-online-platforms))]

Policy direction: obligations should track reach, recommendation architecture, amplification, targeting, monetization, moderation architecture, and measurable systemic harm. Child-safety design duties, recommendation auditability, and sovereign-scale platform-risk obligations fit this framework more naturally than either the old passive-intermediary fiction or blanket publisher liability. ^[Sources: arXiv, [When Kids Mode Isn't For Kids: Invest...](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.00299) (2025-06-30; see [Appendix A10](#appendix-a10-when-kids-mode-isn-t-for-kids-investigating-tiktok-s-under-13-experience)); arXiv, [Unfiltered: How Teens Engage in Body...](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.02176) (2025-04-02; see [Appendix A13](#appendix-a13-unfiltered-how-teens-engage-in-body-image-and-shaming-discussions-via-instagram-direct-messages-dms)); FTC, [A Look Behind the Screens: Examining...](https://www.ftc.gov/reports/look-behind-screens-examining-data-practices-social-media-video-streaming-services) (n.d.; see [Appendix A4](#appendix-a4-a-look-behind-the-screens-examining-the-data-practices-of-social-media-and-video-streaming-services))]

Interpretation: once that structure is clear, frontier weights cannot be treated as ordinary private property by default. The dossier's strongest normative direction is patrimony-oriented governance: public-trust logic, anti-monopoly limits, democratic oversight, interoperability duties, and restrictions on exclusive commercialization. In its strongest form, this doctrine pushes toward AI treated more like civic utility infrastructure: open and as broadly accessible as public-interest governance, safety constraints, and durable shared infrastructure can make it. This is advocated doctrine, not settled law. ^[Sources: TwinChat, [AI Copyright Weights: A New Frontier...](https://medium.com/twinchat/ai-copyright-weights-a-new-frontier-in-intellectual-property-law-d8ee1b6c55ee) (n.d.; see [Appendix A17](#appendix-a17-ai-copyright-weights-a-new-frontier-in-intellectual-property-law)); U.S. House of Representatives, [Bipartisan House Task Force Report on...](https://www.speaker.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/AI-Task-Force-Report-FINAL.pdf) (2024-12-17; see [Appendix A18](#appendix-a18-bipartisan-house-task-force-report-on-artificial-intelligence)); U.S. Copyright Office, [Copyright and Artificial Intelligence...](https://copyright.gov/ai/Copyright-and-Artificial-Intelligence-Part-3-Generative-AI-Training-Report-Pre-Publication-Version.pdf) (2025-05-09; see [Appendix A6](#appendix-a6-copyright-and-artificial-intelligence-part-3-generative-ai-training-pre-publication-version)); U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California via CourtListener, [Bartz et al. v. Policy direction: transparency around material training inputs, anti-enclosure limits for dominant model holders, democratic governance mechanisms, and restrictions on exclusive commercialization where public support is material belong inside this reform. ^[Sources: U.S. Copyright Office, [Copyright and Artificial Intelligence...](https://copyright.gov/ai/Copyright-and-Artificial-Intelligence-Part-3-Generative-AI-Training-Report-Pre-Publication-Version.pdf) (2025-05-09; see [Appendix A6](#appendix-a6-copyright-and-artificial-intelligence-part-3-generative-ai-training-pre-publication-version)); OpenAI, [OpenAI’s proposals for the U.S. AI Ac...](https://openai.com/global-affairs/openai-proposals-for-the-us-ai-action-plan/) (2025-03-13; see [Appendix A20](#appendix-a20-openais-proposals-for-the-us-ai-action-plan)); AP, [Reddit strikes $60M deal allowing Goo...](https://apnews.com/article/a7f131c7cb4225307134ef21d3c6a708) (2024-02-22; see [Appendix A16](#appendix-a16-reddit-strikes-60m-deal-allowing-google-to-train-ai-models-on-its-posts-unveils-ipo-plans))]

### Limit Government Capture by AI Companies

Documented problem: frontier firms already participate in policy formation, export-control protection, government-only offerings, classified-environment distribution channels, lobbying, electoral vehicles, and military-adjacent personnel structures. ^[Sources: OpenAI, [OpenAI’s proposals for the U.S. AI Ac...](https://openai.com/global-affairs/openai-proposals-for-the-us-ai-action-plan/) (2025-03-13; see [Appendix A20](#appendix-a20-openais-proposals-for-the-us-ai-action-plan)); OpenAI, [Introducing OpenAI for Government](https://openai.com/global-affairs/introducing-openai-for-government/) (2025-06-16; see [Appendix A7](#appendix-a7-introducing-openai-for-government)); Anthropic, [Claude Gov models for U.S. national s...](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-gov-models-for-u-s-national-security-customers) (2025-06-06; see [Appendix A8](#appendix-a8-claude-gov-models-for-u-s-national-security-customers)); Palantir Technologies, [Anthropic and Palantir Partner to Bri...](https://investors.palantir.com/news-details/2024/Anthropic-and-Palantir-Partner-to-Bring-Claude-AI-Models-to-AWS-for-U.S.-Government-Intelligence-and-Defense-Operations/) (2024-11-07; see [Appendix A21](#appendix-a21-anthropic-and-palantir-partner-to-bring-claude-ai-models-to-aws-for-u-s-government-intelligence-and-defense-operations)); U.S. Army Public Affairs, [Army Launches Detachment 201: Executi...](https://www.army.mil/article/286317/army_launches_detachment_201_executive_innovation_corps_to_drive_tech_transformation) (2025-06-13; see [Appendix A9](#appendix-a9-army-launches-detachment-201-executive-innovation-corps-to-drive-tech-transformation)); Axios, [Anthropic outspends OpenAI in biggest...](https://www.axios.com/2026/04/21/anthropic-outspends-openai-biggest-lobbying-quarter) (2026-04-21; see [Appendix A22](#appendix-a22-anthropic-outspends-openai-in-biggest-ever-lobbying-quarter))]

Interpretation: any frontier firm benefiting materially from public funding, procurement, compute support, export-control protection, regulatory protection, or national-security access should face heightened public obligations. Public support should increase transparency duties rather than deepen private enclosure. This is an anti-capture doctrine, not a generic appeal to ethics and not a claim of total hidden control. ^[Sources: OpenAI, [Introducing OpenAI for Government](https://openai.com/global-affairs/introducing-openai-for-government/) (2025-06-16; see [Appendix A7](#appendix-a7-introducing-openai-for-government)); Anthropic, [Claude Gov models for U.S. national s...](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-gov-models-for-u-s-national-security-customers) (2025-06-06; see [Appendix A8](#appendix-a8-claude-gov-models-for-u-s-national-security-customers)); Federal Election Commission, [LEADING THE FUTURE - committee overview](https://www.fec.gov/data/committee/C00916114/) (2026-03-31; see [Appendix A23](#appendix-a23-leading-the-future-committee-overview)); Dario Amodei, [The Adolescence of Technology](https://darioamodei.com/essay/the-adolescence-of-technology) (2026-01-01; see [Appendix A24](#appendix-a24-the-adolescence-of-technology))]

Policy direction: procurement visibility, subsidy transparency, lobbying disclosure, conflict-of-interest rules, revolving-door limits, independent audits, model-risk reporting, and limits on exclusive private benefit from publicly supported AI infrastructure should be the baseline. Where firms are simultaneously building quasi-public intelligence infrastructure and private survivability hedges, the burden for public accountability should rise further: dependency-building should not be paired with private exemption from shared failure without stronger oversight and obligations. ^[Sources: AP, [FTC opens inquiry into Big Tech's par...](https://apnews.com/article/67feef411ef311f0be543f546ef34b3d) (2024-01-25; see [Appendix A25](#appendix-a25-ftc-opens-inquiry-into-big-tech-s-partnerships-with-leading-ai-startups)); OpenAI, [Introducing OpenAI for Government](https://openai.com/global-affairs/introducing-openai-for-government/) (2025-06-16; see [Appendix A7](#appendix-a7-introducing-openai-for-government)); Anthropic, [Claude Gov models for U.S. national s...](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-gov-models-for-u-s-national-security-customers) (2025-06-06; see [Appendix A8](#appendix-a8-claude-gov-models-for-u-s-national-security-customers)); U.S. Army Public Affairs, [Army Launches Detachment 201: Executi...](https://www.army.mil/article/286317/army_launches_detachment_201_executive_innovation_corps_to_drive_tech_transformation) (2025-06-13; see [Appendix A9](#appendix-a9-army-launches-detachment-201-executive-innovation-corps-to-drive-tech-transformation)); Tad Friend, [Sam Altman's Manifest Destiny](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2016/10/10/sam-altmans-manifest-destiny) (2016-10-03; see [Appendix A26](#appendix-a26-sam-altman-s-manifest-destiny)); Makena Kelly, [Inside Mark Zuckerberg’s Top-Secret H...](https://www.wired.com/story/mark-zuckerberg-inside-hawaii-compound) (2023-12-14; see [Appendix A27](#appendix-a27-inside-mark-zuckerbergs-top-secret-hawaii-compound)); Rebecca Rose, [New Zealand woos the wealthy with ‘go...](https://www.ft.com/content/76ca455c-3b16-4413-8738-3f17431d125b) (2025-09-24; see [Appendix A28](#appendix-a28-new-zealand-woos-the-wealthy-with-golden-visa-changes))]
