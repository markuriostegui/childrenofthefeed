# Source Policy

## Hierarchy

- `T1`: official documents, courts, laws, filings, papers, regulatory reports.
- `T2`: Reuters, AP, FT, NYT, WSJ, Bloomberg, Nature, Brookings, CFR, CSIS.
- `T3`: interviews, podcasts, and YouTube transcripts only when they function as primary sources and are corroborated.

## Exclusions

The following will not be used as factual support:

- conspiratorial media
- neighborhood blogs
- anonymous accounts
- untraceable aggregators

## Editorial Rule

Every sensitive claim must be frozen with an exact date, exact URL, and evidence level.

State-company interaction claims require extra discipline: use exact dates, exact source boundaries, and formal records or high-credibility reporting for lobbying, procurement, subsidies, regulatory participation, defense ties, and public-benefit restructuring.
