# Children of the Feed V2 Remaining Asset Prompts

This document defines the remaining literary-publication assets needed to bring every chapter to the required density:

- `3–5` narrative illustrations per chapter
- `1–2` vivid branded infographics per chapter

All prompts are designed for `gpt-image-2`, English-first, and aligned to the established brand system:

- photoreal cinematic realism for narrative scenes
- prestige-magazine / YouTube-thumbnail immediacy for hero compositions
- dark technofeudal palette
- ember red, dirty gold, pale bone, deep black
- no readable text in image
- no logos
- no typographic artifacts
- dynamic composition with a clear focal subject

Important:

- the narrative-scene prompts in this file remain active
- the infographic prompts in this file are now superseded by `book/assets/prompts/v3_infographic_text_prompts.md`
- all active literary infographics now require readable quoted text labels
