# Children of the Feed Brand Guide

## Core identity

This literary branch should feel like a prestige magazine, a political lament, and a controlled generational indictment at the same time.

It is not an academic export in better clothes. It is a commercial and public-reading edition built from the dossier's evidence spine.

## Tone

- cinematic
- wounded but controlled
- mythic without becoming irrational
- political without becoming partisan
- accessible without becoming generic
- dark without becoming hopeless

## Visual vocabulary

- ember red
- dirty gold
- pale bone
- deep black
- black-glass interfaces
- cathedral light
- chain, eye, altar, scroll, notification ghosts, digital ruins

## Recurrent motifs

- the feed as land
- the platform as lord
- the user as serf
- the algorithm as law
- data as harvest
- AI as the speaking monument built from the harvest

## Reader promise

The literary edition should make the reader feel recognized before it asks them to think structurally.

It should say:

- you are not weak for being addicted
- you are not crazy for feeling broken
- you are not obsolete because AI imitates your labor
- you were born into an extraction system

## Evidence rule

The prose may be more forceful than the papers, but it cannot become looser than the papers.

- strong factual claims still come from the chapter papers
- disputed matters stay marked as disputed
- broader synthesis remains clearly interpretive
- no new conspiracy language enters through style alone
