Title: Interactions Between Artificial Intelligence and Digital Public Infrastructure: Concepts, Benefits, and Challenges
Publisher: arXiv
Published at: 2024-12-07

Working extract:
This paper examines how artificial intelligence can interact with digital public infrastructure and public-service systems. It frames the issue in terms of public value, interoperability, governance, inclusion, and privacy. The abstract positions AI in public infrastructure as a design choice that can produce benefits, but only under accountable standards and institutional safeguards.
