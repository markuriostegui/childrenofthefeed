Title: FTC opens inquiry into Big Tech's partnerships with leading AI startups
Publisher: AP
Published at: 2024-01-25

Working extract:
AP reported that the FTC opened a compulsory-order inquiry into major AI investments and partnerships involving large technology firms and leading AI startups. The article said the agency sought records from Alphabet, Amazon, Anthropic, Microsoft, and OpenAI to understand whether capital ties, cloud dependence, and distribution partnerships were reinforcing concentration in the AI market.
