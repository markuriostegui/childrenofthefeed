# ¿Cómo hackear al tecnofeudalismo?

## Factual Summary

- This June 29, 2026 Dinero en Imagen column by Paul Lara argues for an anti-technofeudal response to concentrated platform and AI power.
- It explicitly treats digital platforms as privately owned territories on which users generate value without owning the infrastructure or the upside.
- The column uses Mariana Mazzucato's common-good framework to move from diagnosis to institutional response, including public conditionality, participation, and digital sovereignty.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- The piece says big tech firms are not merely selling products but owning "digital lands" worked by users through clicks, data, and prompts.
- It argues that the current risk is mission-washing: public money and social rhetoric used while private firms keep the upside, algorithms, and extracted data.
