Social Media Seen as Mostly Good for Democracy Across Many Nations, But U.S. is a Major Outlier
Source: Pew Research Center
Published: 2022-12-06
URL: https://www.pewresearch.org/global/2022/12/06/social-media-seen-as-mostly-good-for-democracy-across-many-nations-but-u-s-is-a-major-outlier/

Normalized extract:
- Pew reports substantial cross-national differences, with the United States as a clear outlier: 34% of U.S. adults say social media has been good for democracy, while 64% say it has had a bad impact.
- Across the 19 countries surveyed, a median of 84% say access to the internet and social media has made people easier to manipulate with false information and rumors.
- A median of 65% say social media has made people more divided in their political opinions. More than four in ten say it has made people less civil in how they talk about politics.
- In the U.S., 79% say internet and social media have made people more divided in their political opinions, and 69% say they have made people less civil in how they talk about politics.
- Pew also reports that many respondents still see social media as effective for raising awareness and influencing policy, which matters because the same systems are seen as both empowering and corrosive.
