# Social media algorithms 'amplifying misogynistic content'

## Factual Summary

- The Guardian reported on February 6, 2024 that researchers from University College London and the University of Kent found TikTok's recommendation system rapidly amplified misogynistic content.
- The article says the researchers observed a four-fold increase in misogynistic recommendations over a five-day monitoring period.
- The piece is useful as a serious secondary source on algorithmic norm-shaping and youth-relevant exposure dynamics, even though it is not the primary research paper itself.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- The article says extreme content was being pushed on young people and becoming normalized.
- Researchers detected a four-fold increase in the level of misogynistic content suggested by TikTok over five days.
- The reporting frames the issue as algorithmic amplification rather than only as user choice.
