Relevant extracted findings from the GPT-2 paper:
- The paper states: "Instead, we created a new web scrape which emphasizes document quality."
- It then explains that the team "scraped all outbound links from Reddit, a social media platform, which received at least 3 karma."
- The paper says this heuristic can function as an indicator of whether other users found the link "interesting, educational, or just funny."
- It reports that "The resulting dataset, WebText, contains the text subset of these 45 million links."
- It further states that after filtering and de-duplication, the preliminary version contained "slightly over 8 million documents" totaling "40 GB of text."
