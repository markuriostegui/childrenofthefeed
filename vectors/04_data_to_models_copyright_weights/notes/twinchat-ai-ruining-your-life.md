- This essay defines the dossier’s broadest narrative architecture: degradation of everyday life, extraction of human value, and the construction of technical empire.

- It should be used as editorial orientation, not as independent proof for disputed facts.

## What It Suggests

- That degraded social experience is not an isolated side effect, but a condition of possibility for the new AI economy.
