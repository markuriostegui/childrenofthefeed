# Mark Zuckerberg 'personally authorized' Meta's copyright infringement, publishers allege

## Factual Summary

AP reports that on May 5, 2026, five major publishers and author Scott Turow filed a class-action lawsuit against Meta and Mark Zuckerberg in federal court in Manhattan. The complaint alleges that Meta used millions of copyrighted books and journal articles to train Llama without permission or compensation and that Zuckerberg personally authorized the conduct. Meta responded that it would fight the suit aggressively and argued that AI training can qualify as fair use.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- AP describes the filing as a class-action lawsuit brought by major publishers and an author against Meta and Zuckerberg.
- The article reports allegations that Meta used millions of copyrighted works to train Llama without permission or payment.
- AP also records Meta's response that it would fight the case aggressively and that AI training may fall within fair use under current law.
