# Queue

## High Priority

- Serious sources on training data, copyright, fair use, and model weights.
- Official filings, lawsuits, or policy submissions on learning from copyrighted material.
- Sources that connect extracted human labor to frontier AI products.

## Medium Priority

- Technical explainers or papers on model training and distillation.
- Corroborated interviews from major labs and legal experts.

## Low Priority

- General opinion pieces with no independent evidentiary value.
