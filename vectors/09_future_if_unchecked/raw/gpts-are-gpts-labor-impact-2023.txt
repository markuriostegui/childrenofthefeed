Title: GPTs are GPTs: An Early Look at the Labor Market Impact Potential of Large Language Models
Publisher: arXiv
Published at: 2023-03-17

Working extract:
This paper maps large language model capabilities onto occupational tasks in the U.S. labor market. It argues that exposure is broad and not confined to low-wage routine work. The analysis emphasizes that task exposure is not the same thing as total job loss, but it does show that many professional and white-collar occupations contain language-intensive tasks that can be accelerated, reorganized, or partially automated by LLM systems.
