Title: Gen-AI: Artificial Intelligence and the Future of Work
Publisher: IMF
Published at: 2024-01-14

Working extract:
The IMF note states that artificial intelligence can reshape the global economy and labor markets, with advanced economies likely to face the benefits and disruptions sooner because of their concentration of cognitive-intensive jobs. It highlights recurring patterns of exposure by income, education, and gender and argues that policy choices will strongly influence whether AI amplifies inequality or supports transition and productivity.
