# Meta Platforms, Inc. 2022 Definitive Proxy Statement

## Factual Summary

- Meta's April 8, 2022 proxy statement documents the state of the board after the Facebook-to-Meta rebrand and before the 2022 annual meeting.
- The proxy includes Mark Zuckerberg's statement thanking Peter Thiel for service on the board since 2005 and saying Thiel would retire when his term ended at the annual meeting.
- The document also records that Thiel was still serving as chair of Meta's compensation, nominating and governance committee at that moment.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- Zuckerberg's letter says Peter Thiel would retire from the board when his term concluded at the annual meeting.
- The same letter thanks Thiel for service on the board since 2005.
- The proxy states that Peter A. Thiel currently served as chair of Meta's compensation, nominating and governance committee.
- The filing places this governance continuity in the same document that frames the company's post-rebrand transition into the Meta era.

## What It Proves

- That Peter Thiel's formal role at Facebook / Meta lasted well beyond the early startup period and remained active into 2022.
- That the company itself presented Thiel's board tenure as spanning from 2005 to the end of his term at the 2022 annual meeting.
- That Thiel still held committee-level governance authority at Meta immediately before his departure from the board.
- That continuity between the Facebook surveillance era and the Meta rebrand era included continuity in elite board membership.

## What It Suggests

- That Thiel's connection to Facebook / Meta should be treated as a durable governance relationship rather than a one-time seed investment anecdote.
- That platform-era capital networks carried forward into Meta's later strategic phases instead of disappearing once Facebook matured.
- That Chapter 8 can discuss continuity of elite legitimacy networks using documented board structure rather than insinuation.

## What It Does Not Prove

- It does not prove the reasons for Thiel's departure beyond what the proxy states.
- It does not prove that Thiel dictated Meta's rebrand, metaverse strategy, or later AI posture.
- It does not prove guilt by association between Meta and any other actor connected to Thiel.
