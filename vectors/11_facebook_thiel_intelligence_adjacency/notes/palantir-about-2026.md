# About Palantir

- Palantir's official About page presents the company as a mission-driven builder of platforms for integrating, securing, and analyzing data.
- The page frames Palantir's work around hard institutional problems, machine-assisted analysis, privacy tradeoffs, and support for powerful institutions handling sensitive information.
- This source is useful for documenting how Palantir publicly defines its own institutional role, not for proving any hidden relationship.

- The page says Palantir was founded because existing products were too rigid for novel problems and too slow to deploy.
