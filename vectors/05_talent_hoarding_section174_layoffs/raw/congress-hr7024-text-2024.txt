Relevant extracted lines:
- "there shall be allowed as a deduction any domestic research or experimental expenditures which are paid or incurred by the taxpayer during the taxable year."
- Taxpayers may elect to charge such expenditures to capital account and be allowed an amortization deduction ratably over a period of not less than 60 months.
- "For purposes of this section, any amount paid or incurred in connection with the development of any software shall be treated as a research or experimental expenditure."
- The section would not apply to amounts paid or incurred in taxable years beginning after December 31, 2025.
- Passed the House of Representatives January 31, 2024.
