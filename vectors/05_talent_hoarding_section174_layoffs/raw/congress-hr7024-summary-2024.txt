- Title II -- American Innovation and Growth.
- This title allows taxpayers to delay the date on which they must begin deducting their domestic research or experimental research costs over a five-year period until 2026.
- Taxpayers may therefore expense such costs incurred between 2022-2026.
- The title extends the allowance for depreciation, amortization, or depletion in determining the limitation of the business interest deduction.
