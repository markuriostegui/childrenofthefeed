# Meta posts lower Q4 profit, announces huge stock buyback

## Factual Summary

- AP reported on February 1, 2023 that Meta's revenue had declined for a third consecutive quarter while the company was still digesting its November 2022 layoffs.
- The report explicitly ties those layoffs to pandemic-era aggressive hiring, slowing growth after lockdowns, and investor concern over metaverse spending.
- This makes the article useful for the dossier's narrower claim that layoffs emerged from a structural correction already underway before AI became the dominant public explanation.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- AP says Meta had laid off 11,000 workers, about 13% of its workforce, in November 2022.
- The report says Mark Zuckerberg blamed the layoffs on aggressive hiring during the pandemic and the later slowdown once lockdown behavior faded.
- AP quotes Zuckerberg saying 2023 would be Meta's "Year of Efficiency."
- The same article says Reality Labs posted a fourth-quarter operating loss of $4.28 billion.

## What It Proves

- That a major tech company publicly linked layoffs to pandemic overhiring and slowing post-lockdown growth.
- That efficiency rhetoric was already being layered onto a revenue slowdown and investor pressure over unprofitable strategic bets.
- That metaverse losses were part of the financial backdrop to Meta's workforce correction.

## What It Suggests
