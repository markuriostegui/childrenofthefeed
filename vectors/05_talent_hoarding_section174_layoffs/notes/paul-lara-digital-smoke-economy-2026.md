# La economía del humo digital

## Factual Summary

- This June 8, 2026 Paul Lara column argues that generative AI may be producing more symbolic output than durable economic value.
- It focuses on the gap between exploding quantities of AI-generated content and the weak evidence of corresponding real-world productivity, revenue, or GDP gains.
- The piece uses the term `slop` and the metaphor of digital smoke to describe a system capable of manufacturing noise faster than wealth.

- The column says that never has so much content, code, and synthetic production been generated while it remains so difficult to prove corresponding real wealth creation.
- It argues that the abundance problem raises the search cost of useful knowledge rather than automatically increasing efficiency.
- It frames AI output inflation as a possible new tax on human time.
