# Judge denies Elon Musk's request to block OpenAI for-profit conversion but welcomes trial

## Factual Summary

- This Associated Press report states that, on March 5, 2025, U.S. District Judge Yvonne Gonzalez Rogers denied Elon Musk's request for a court order blocking OpenAI from converting itself to a for-profit company.
- The report also states that the judge offered to hold a trial as soon as fall 2025, which means the broader dispute remained live even though the emergency request failed.
- As used in the dossier, the source matters because it provides a stronger open procedural account than the paywalled FT layer and includes bounded judicial language about the injunction standard and public-interest stakes.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- AP reports that the judge ruled Musk had "not demonstrated likelihood of success on the merits" for preliminary relief.
- AP reports that the court was willing to move quickly "given the public interest at stake and potential for harm if a conversion contrary to law occurred."
- AP reports that OpenAI welcomed the decision and that Musk's lawyer welcomed the offer of an expedited trial.
