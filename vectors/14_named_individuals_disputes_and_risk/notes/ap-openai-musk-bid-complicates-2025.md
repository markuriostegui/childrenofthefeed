# How Elon Musk $97.4 billion bid complicates matters for OpenAI

## Factual Summary

- On February 11, 2025, AP reported that Elon Musk's $97.4 billion bid complicated OpenAI's effort to convert into a more conventional for-profit structure.
- The article recorded Altman's response that OpenAI was not for sale and restated the company's mission language about ensuring AGI benefits all humanity.
- AP framed the dispute as a structurally important fight over nonprofit purpose, sale constraints, and control, not as proof that either Musk or Altman was right on the merits.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- Altman said OpenAI was not for sale.
- AP reported that legal scholars emphasized OpenAI's legally binding charitable purpose as a constraint on any sale.
- The piece described Musk as an early investor and former board member who was now challenging OpenAI's direction while building xAI.
