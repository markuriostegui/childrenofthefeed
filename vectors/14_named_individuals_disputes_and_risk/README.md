# Named individuals, disputes, and risk

## ID

`14_named_individuals_disputes_and_risk`

## Target Chapters

- `08`

## Working Thesis

Sensitive cases only when they have structural relevance and sufficient support.

## Guiding Questions

- What is firmly documented?
- What is disputed?
- Which connections should be treated as hypotheses?

## Exclusions

- Do not turn conjectures into facts.
- Do not use excluded outlets as factual support.
- Do not lose date and source traceability.

## Key Disputes

- Which named disputes materially clarify governance structure, mission conflict, conversion pressure, or public-interest stakes.
- How to keep procedurally live allegations relevant without treating them as established truth.
- How to preserve exact allegation status, response, and procedural posture while excluding scandal collage and guilt by association.
