- AP reports that U.S. District Judge Yvonne Gonzalez Rogers denied Elon Musk's request for a court order blocking OpenAI from converting itself to a for-profit company.
- AP reports that the judge ruled late Tuesday that "Musk has not demonstrated likelihood of success on the merits" for a preliminary injunction.
- AP reports that the judge offered to hold a trial as soon as fall 2025 "given the public interest at stake and potential for harm if a conversion contrary to law occurred."
- AP reports that OpenAI welcomed the ruling while Musk's attorney said he was pleased that the court offered an expedited trial on the core claims.
