# DeepSeek-R1: Incentivizing Reasoning Capability in LLMs via Reinforcement Learning

## Factual Summary

- First published on arXiv on January 22, 2025, the DeepSeek-R1 paper introduces `DeepSeek-R1-Zero` and `DeepSeek-R1` as first-generation reasoning models.
- The abstract says `R1-Zero` was trained via large-scale reinforcement learning without supervised fine-tuning as a preliminary step, while `R1` adds cold-start data and multi-stage training.
- The authors claim `DeepSeek-R1` achieves performance comparable to `OpenAI-o1-1217` on reasoning tasks and that the models were open-sourced.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- arXiv lists the paper's publication date as January 22, 2025.
- The abstract says `DeepSeek-R1-Zero` demonstrates strong reasoning behavior through reinforcement learning alone but suffers from readability and language-mixing issues.
- The paper says `DeepSeek-R1` incorporates cold-start data before reinforcement learning and reaches performance comparable to `OpenAI-o1-1217` on reasoning tasks.
- The abstract says DeepSeek open-sourced `R1-Zero`, `R1`, and six distilled dense models.
