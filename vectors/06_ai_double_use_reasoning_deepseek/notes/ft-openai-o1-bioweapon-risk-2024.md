# OpenAI acknowledges new models increase risk of misuse to create bioweapons

## Factual Summary

- On September 13, 2024, the Financial Times reported that OpenAI said its newly released `o1` models had meaningfully increased misuse risk in the biological domain.
- The report centers on OpenAI's own system-card language and the company's launch framing around stronger reasoning in science and technical tasks.
- It is useful to Chapter 06 because it documents a contemporaneous moment where higher-capability release and higher-risk acknowledgement were public at the same time.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- The article is dated September 13, 2024.
- FT reports that OpenAI's latest models had "meaningfully" increased the risk of misuse to create biological weapons.
- The piece says OpenAI released `o1` while also describing improved reasoning on math and scientific tasks as a breakthrough toward AGI.
- FT reports that the system card gave the new models a medium risk for chemical, biological, radiological, and nuclear misuse.

## What It Proves

- That serious `T2` reporting documented OpenAI publicly acknowledging higher misuse risk at the same moment it was releasing stronger reasoning models.

## What It Suggests
