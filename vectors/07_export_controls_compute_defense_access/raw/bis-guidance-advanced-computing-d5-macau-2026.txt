Verified excerpt points from Codex web capture:
- PDF heading date: May 31, 2026.
- BIS says a license is required to export advanced computing items to entities headquartered in Country Group D:5 or Macau even if the entities are outside those destinations.
- BIS says the requirement was first introduced on November 17, 2023.
- BIS says that in January 2025 the AI Diffusion Rule transferred the requirement for certain ".a" items from section 744.23(a) into section 742.6 as part of a new worldwide license requirement.
- BIS says its May 2025 non-enforcement policy for the AI Diffusion Rule's new compliance requirements does not cancel the earlier requirement for D:5- or Macau-linked transactions.
- BIS says exporters should continue to seek licenses for such transactions unless a listed license exception applies.
