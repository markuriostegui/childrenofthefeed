# Amazon and Anthropic Announce Strategic Collaboration to Advance Generative AI

## Factual Summary

- On September 25, 2023, Amazon announced a strategic collaboration with Anthropic.
- The release says AWS would become Anthropic's primary cloud provider for mission-critical workloads, including safety research and future foundation model development.
- It also says Anthropic would use AWS Trainium and Inferentia chips for future model training and deployment, while Amazon would invest up to $4 billion in Anthropic.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- The page is dated September 25, 2023.
- The subheading says Anthropic selected AWS as its primary cloud provider and would train and deploy future foundation models on AWS Trainium and Inferentia chips.
- The body says Anthropic will use AWS Trainium and Inferentia chips to build, train, and deploy future foundation models.
- It says AWS will become Anthropic's primary cloud provider for mission-critical workloads, including safety research and future foundation model development.
- The release says Anthropic plans to run the majority of its workloads on AWS.
- It says Amazon will invest up to $4 billion in Anthropic and take a minority ownership position.
