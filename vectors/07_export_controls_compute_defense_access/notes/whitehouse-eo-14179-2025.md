# Removing Barriers to American Leadership in Artificial Intelligence

## Factual Summary

- On January 23, 2025, the White House published the order “Removing Barriers to American Leadership in Artificial Intelligence.”
- The text establishes as official U.S. policy the goal to “sustain and enhance America’s global AI dominance” and orders agencies to review, suspend, revise, or rescind actions derived from EO 14110 when they create obstacles.
- It also instructs the creation of an AI Action Plan within 180 days, coordinated by presidential advisers on science, national security, and economic policy.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- The order is dated January 23, 2025.
- Section 2 states that official policy is to sustain and enhance U.S. global dominance in AI.
- Section 5 orders review of measures taken under EO 14110 and suspension, revision, or rescission of those inconsistent with the new policy.
- Section 4 directs the development of an AI Action Plan.

## What It Proves

- That competition over AI was officially framed by the U.S. government in terms of global dominance, economic competitiveness, and national security.
- That there was an explicit turn toward a more deregulatory line aimed at removing internal barriers for U.S. actors.
- That the 2025 state framework directly connected AI policy, national security, and administrative reordering.

## What It Suggests

- That the language of technological leadership already functions as a doctrine of state power rather than only as a business vision.
- That frontier labs operate in an environment where market advantage and national strategy reinforce one another.

## What It Does Not Prove

- It does not prove total government control over models or private labs.
- It does not prove concrete material results of the later AI Action Plan; it only sets political direction and an institutional mechanism.
- It does not prove that all prior safety-oriented measures lacked merit.
