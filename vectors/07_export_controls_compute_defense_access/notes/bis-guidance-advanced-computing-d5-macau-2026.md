# Guidance Regarding Enforcement of License Requirements for Advanced Computing Items for Entities Headquartered in Country Group D:5 and Macau

## Factual Summary

- On May 31, 2026, BIS issued a one-page guidance PDF clarifying that a license is still required to export advanced computing items to entities headquartered in Country Group D:5 or Macau, even when those entities are located outside those destinations.
- The guidance states that this license requirement was first introduced on November 17, 2023 and that the January 2025 AI Diffusion Rule moved the requirement for certain advanced computing items into a new worldwide license framework under the EAR.
- BIS also says its May 2025 non-enforcement posture toward the AI Diffusion Rule's new compliance requirements does not eliminate the earlier license requirement for these D:5 and Macau-linked transactions.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- The PDF is dated May 31, 2026.
- BIS says the guidance is being issued "to clarify that a license is required to export advanced computing items" to entities headquartered in Country Group D:5 or Macau even if they are outside those destinations.
- The guidance states: "This license requirement was first introduced on November 17, 2023."
- It says: "In January 2025, the AI Diffusion Rule transferred the requirement for these '.a' items from § 744.23(a) into § 742.6 as part of a new worldwide license requirement."
- BIS says its non-enforcement policy for the AI Diffusion Rule's new compliance requirements does not cancel the preexisting requirement for D:5- and Macau-headquartered entities.

## What It Does Not Prove
