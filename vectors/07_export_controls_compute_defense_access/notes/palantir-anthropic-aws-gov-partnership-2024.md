# Anthropic and Palantir Partner to Bring Claude AI Models to AWS for U.S. Government Intelligence and Defense Operations

## Factual Summary

- Published on November 7, 2024, Palantir's investor-relations release announces a partnership among Anthropic, Palantir, and Amazon Web Services to provide U.S. intelligence and defense agencies access to the Claude `3` and `3.5` model family.
- The page says Claude would be operationalized inside Palantir `AIP` on AWS, including an `Impact Level 6` (`IL6`) accredited environment associated with strict defense-security requirements.
- The release frames model access, cloud hosting, accreditation, and defense-intelligence use cases as a bundled distribution stack rather than separate services.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- The release is dated `11 / 07 / 2024`.
- Palantir says the partnership provides U.S. intelligence and defense agencies access to Claude `3` and `3.5` on AWS.
- The release says Claude became accessible within Palantir `AIP` on AWS earlier that month.
- Palantir says it is the `first industry partner to bring Claude models to classified environments`.
- The page highlights `DISA IL6` accreditation and AWS public-sector infrastructure as key parts of the delivery environment.

## What It Proves

- That by November 7, 2024 Anthropic model access for U.S. intelligence and defense agencies was being publicly distributed through a Palantir-plus-AWS stack.
- That classified-environment frontier-model access was tied to specific accredited infrastructure and defense-cleared platform arrangements rather than open public availability.
- That Chapter 07 can document a concrete distribution channel where model access, cloud concentration, accreditation, and defense use cases are bundled together.

## What It Suggests

- That access control at the high end increasingly depends on cleared intermediaries and accredited cloud environments, not only on model ownership.
- That anti-capture analysis should pay attention to which firms become gatekeepers for state use of frontier models even when they are not the model developer.
- That the same AI system can diffuse publicly at one layer while becoming more tightly structured and permissioned inside government channels.

## What It Does Not Prove

- It does not prove how extensively intelligence and defense customers actually used the announced stack after launch.
- It does not prove that Palantir or AWS controlled Anthropic's broader public policy positions.
- It does not prove unified backstage control; it proves a formal, accredited pathway for government access to frontier models.

## Reliability

- Primary Palantir investor-relations release.
- Reliable for date, named partners, stated deployment environment, and the public distribution framing for government customers.
- Because it is a corporate press release, claims about impact and advantage should be treated as issuer statements rather than independent performance verification.
