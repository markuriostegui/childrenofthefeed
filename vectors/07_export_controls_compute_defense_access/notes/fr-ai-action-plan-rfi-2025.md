# Request for Information on the Development of an Artificial Intelligence (AI) Action Plan

## Factual Summary

- The Federal Register published Notice 2025-02305 (90 FR 9088) on February 6, 2025, issued by the National Science Foundation on behalf of OSTP and the NITRD NCO.
- The notice requests public comment for the development of the AI Action Plan ordered by the White House and defines that plan as an instrument for sustaining and enhancing U.S. dominance in AI without unnecessary regulatory barriers.
- The source documents the administrative step that translates the presidential order into a formal process of consultation and public-policy design.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- The notice identifies the document as 2025-02305 (90 FR 9088) and dates it February 6, 2025.
- It states that the plan derives from the presidential order signed on January 23, 2025.
- It says the AI Action Plan will define the priority actions needed to sustain and enhance U.S. dominance in AI.
- The comment deadline was set for March 15, 2025.

## What It Proves

- That the language of AI dominance did not remain only in a political order, but moved into an official federal consultation process.
- That OSTP and federal agencies activated a formal channel to gather input from industry, academia, and other actors on U.S. AI strategy.
- That the dossier can document a clear institutional sequence between the presidential order, the RFI, and later corporate policy positioning.

## What It Suggests

- That U.S. AI policy in 2025 was being organized as a coordinated state agenda rather than as a casual sum of isolated initiatives.
- That firms with lobbying capacity and response speed had a privileged window to influence the design of the plan.

## What It Does Not Prove

- It does not prove which concrete recommendations were ultimately adopted.
- It does not prove favoritism toward a specific company.
- It does not prove a closed regime of model access on its own; it proves the formalization of a power strategy.
