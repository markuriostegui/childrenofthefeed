# The Facebook Company Is Now Meta

## Factual Summary

- Meta's October 28, 2021 rebrand announcement presents the company as moving beyond 2D screens toward the metaverse while consolidating apps and technologies under a new corporate identity.
- The statement also says that, starting with fourth-quarter 2021 results, Meta would report two operating segments: Family of Apps and Reality Labs.
- For the dossier, this source is important because it provides the company's own declared rationale for the rebrand and the financial architecture that separated the core advertising business from the metaverse bet.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- The article is dated October 28, 2021.
- It says Meta's focus will be to bring the metaverse to life and help people connect, find communities, and grow businesses.
- It states that financial reporting would shift to two operating segments: Family of Apps and Reality Labs.
- The piece also says the announcement does not affect how the company uses or shares data.

## What It Proves

- That the rebrand was formally tied to a metaverse-centered strategic narrative, not merely a cosmetic name change.
- That Meta itself created a reporting split capable of distinguishing its mature platform business from its speculative immersive-technology investment.
- That the company did not present the rebrand as a data-governance reset.

## What It Suggests

- That the Meta pivot can be read as an attempt to redirect attention, valuation logic, and future expectations toward a new technological horizon after years of reputational and regulatory damage.
- That the reporting split made it easier for investors and critics alike to track how much the metaverse bet was costing.

## What It Does Not Prove

- It does not prove the rebrand was designed to erase Cambridge Analytica or any single scandal.
- It does not prove the metaverse strategy was insincere.
- It does not prove that the rebrand itself changed underlying surveillance practices.
