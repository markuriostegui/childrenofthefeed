# Meta Platforms, Inc. 2023 Annual Report (Form 10-K)

## Factual Summary

- Meta's 2023 Form 10-K confirms that the company reports two operating segments, Family of Apps and Reality Labs, and says Reality Labs is expected to continue operating at a loss for the foreseeable future.
- The filing also states that Meta's 2024 investment priorities include both AI and the metaverse, and that 20% of total 2023 costs and expenses were recognized in Reality Labs.
- This makes the filing a core evidentiary source for the narrower claim that the metaverse remained a major capital sink even as Meta emphasized AI and preserved its core ad business.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- The filing says Meta reports financial results for two segments: Family of Apps and Reality Labs.
- It says Meta generates substantially all of its revenue from advertising placements in Family of Apps.
- The document states that 80% of total 2023 costs and expenses were recognized in Family of Apps and 20% in Reality Labs.
- It also says Reality Labs is expected to continue operating at a loss for the foreseeable future.

## What It Proves

- That the metaverse bet was not just branding language; it had a dedicated segment and a continuing loss structure visible in SEC reporting.
- That Meta's core economic engine remained advertising, even while management invested heavily in AI and immersive technologies.
- That the rebrand period can be discussed in material financial terms rather than only as a symbolic communications move.

## What It Suggests

- That the Meta story is best read as a layered one: reputational repair, strategic redirection, and high-cost experimentation funded by a still-dominant surveillance-advertising machine.
- That investor patience for the metaverse project depended on the profitability of the core platform business.

## What It Does Not Prove

- It does not prove why management chose the rebrand when it did.
- It does not prove that the metaverse pivot failed in total; it proves only that losses were expected and persistent.
- It does not prove any direct relation to Cambridge Analytica unless paired with the regulatory record.
