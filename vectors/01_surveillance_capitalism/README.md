# Surveillance capitalism and data capture

## ID

`01_surveillance_capitalism`

## Target Chapters

- `01`
- `10`

## Working Thesis

Social infrastructure turned into behavioral extraction and groundwork for big data, undermining the passive-intermediary logic later used to defend sovereign-scale platforms.

## Guiding Questions

- What is firmly documented?
- What is disputed?
- Which connections should be treated as hypotheses?

## Exclusions

- Do not turn conjectures into facts.
- Do not use excluded outlets as factual support.
- Do not lose date and source traceability.

## Key Disputes

- The regulatory and filing record strongly supports a surveillance-capitalism reading of platform business models, but it does not by itself prove a full documented pipeline from every platform dataset into later frontier-model training.
- Intelligence or venture adjacency should be treated as documented permeability and governance overlap, not as proof of direct clandestine command.
