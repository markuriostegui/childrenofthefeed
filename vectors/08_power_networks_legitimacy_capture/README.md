## Working Thesis

Frontier legitimacy is assembled through governance form, elite board composition, security credentials, policy access, operational state-company interfaces, lobbying, philanthropy, and moral authority. The strongest record supports a bounded anti-capture argument, not hidden-command claims.

## Guiding Questions

- What is firmly documented?
- What is disputed?
- Which connections should be treated as hypotheses?
- Which government, electoral, religious, and governance interfaces are formal rather than merely adjacent?

## Exclusions

- Do not turn conjectures into facts.
- Do not use excluded outlets as factual support.
- Do not lose date and source traceability.

## Key Disputes

- How far the documented legitimacy field extends from ordinary influence into institutional capture.
- How to distinguish formal state-company interfaces from theories of unified hidden control.
- How much weight founder philanthropy, religious uptake, and public moral language should carry in a structural argument.
