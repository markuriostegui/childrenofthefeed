# OpenAI appoints one of Wall Street's most powerful dealmakers to its board

## Factual Summary

- On January 14, 2025, the Financial Times reported that OpenAI appointed Adebayo Ogunlesi to its board.
- The report places the appointment inside OpenAI's restructuring push, its competition-driven expansion, and the continuing aftermath of the 2023 governance crisis.
- Ogunlesi is described as a major infrastructure and finance figure with experience navigating large commercial and regulatory environments.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- The article is dated January 14, 2025.
- FT reports that Ogunlesi joined the board as OpenAI pushed forward with moves to become a for-profit company amid rising competition.
- The piece says the board had already been restructured after Altman's ouster and return.
- FT notes Ogunlesi's role in a large infrastructure fund backed by Microsoft, Nvidia, and Abu Dhabi to build data centers and related power infrastructure.
