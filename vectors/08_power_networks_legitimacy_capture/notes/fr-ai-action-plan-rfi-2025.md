# Request for Information on the Development of an Artificial Intelligence (AI) Action Plan

## Factual Summary

- On February 6, 2025, the Federal Register published the formal request for information used to develop the AI Action Plan ordered by the White House.
- The notice translates the dominance language of the executive order into a structured state consultation process.
- For Vector 08, it matters because it documents the institutional channel through which elite firms, academics, and policy actors could compete to define legitimate U.S. AI strategy.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- The notice is identified as 2025-02305 (90 FR 9088).
- It states that the plan will define the priority actions needed to sustain and enhance U.S. dominance in AI.
- It ties directly back to the January 23, 2025 executive order.
- It opened a formal comment process with a March 15, 2025 deadline.

## What It Proves

- That the state created an official consultation pathway for shaping the 2025 AI doctrine.
- That legitimacy in AI was being organized through formal administrative processes, not only informal lobbying.
- That Chapter 8 can map institutional capture or influence as a documented procedural field.

## What It Suggests

- That actors with speed, reputation, and policy capacity had privileged opportunities to shape the official language of AI leadership.
- That the frontier-AI power network extends through public consultation structures as well as direct company-state relationships.

## What It Does Not Prove

- It does not prove which actors were most influential in the final plan.
- It does not prove that the process was captured by one firm or faction.
- It does not prove a closed access regime by itself.
