# OpenAI appoints former top US cyberwarrior Paul Nakasone to its board of directors

## Factual Summary

- On June 14, 2024, AP reported that OpenAI appointed retired Army General Paul Nakasone, the former head of U.S. Cyber Command and the NSA, to its board of directors.
- AP said Nakasone would also join OpenAI's new Safety and Security Committee and linked the move to the company's effort to protect itself from increasingly sophisticated threats.
- The article explicitly situated the appointment in the aftermath of the 2023 board upheaval and the continuing board rebuild.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- AP reported that Nakasone was appointed both to the board and to the Safety and Security Committee.
- The piece said OpenAI's board was still being rebuilt after the prior board fired Altman and was then replaced.
- AP noted that the safety group replaced an earlier safety team that had been disbanded after several of its leaders quit.
