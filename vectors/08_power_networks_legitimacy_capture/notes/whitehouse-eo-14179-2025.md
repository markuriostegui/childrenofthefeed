# Removing Barriers to American Leadership in Artificial Intelligence

## Factual Summary

- On January 23, 2025, the White House made sustaining and enhancing U.S. global AI dominance an official policy objective.
- The order couples national-security language with an administrative commitment to remove internal regulatory obstacles and produce an AI Action Plan.
- For Vector 08, this source shows that frontier-firm legitimacy is reinforced by a state doctrine that publicly valorizes technological dominance.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- The order is dated January 23, 2025.
- It states that official policy is to sustain and enhance U.S. global AI dominance.
- It directs agencies to review and potentially rescind measures seen as inconsistent with that objective.
- It mandates an AI Action Plan coordinated across science, economic, and national-security leadership.

## What It Proves

- That AI dominance was elevated to explicit state doctrine in 2025.
- That deregulatory and dominance-oriented state framing can serve as an external source of legitimacy for frontier firms.
- That Chapter 8 can ground the idea of institutional capture or mutual reinforcement in official state language rather than metaphor alone.

## What It Suggests

- That corporate and state narratives around AI leadership may increasingly converge around a shared imperial vocabulary.
- That the legitimacy of frontier firms is strengthened when national policy frames their success as a public strategic necessity.

## What It Does Not Prove

- It does not prove specific favoritism toward a particular company.
- It does not prove the eventual outcomes of the Action Plan.
- It does not prove total state control over model access or development.
