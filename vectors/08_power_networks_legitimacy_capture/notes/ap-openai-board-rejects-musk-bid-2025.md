# OpenAI board unanimously rejects Elon Musk's $97.4 billion proposal

## Factual Summary

- AP reported on February 14, 2025 that OpenAI's board rejected Musk's bid for the nonprofit entity controlling OpenAI.
- The article records the dispute as a direct struggle over mission, assets, organizational form, and who gets to define the public-interest meaning of the institution.
- For Vector 08, the source matters because it turns legitimacy into an overt governance conflict rather than a vague reputational question.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- Bret Taylor said OpenAI was not for sale.
- OpenAI's counsel said the proposal was not in the best interests of the mission and was rejected.
- AP says Musk alleged a betrayal of OpenAI's founding nonprofit aims.
- The report notes that a judge was still considering Musk's request and had expressed skepticism about some of his claims.
