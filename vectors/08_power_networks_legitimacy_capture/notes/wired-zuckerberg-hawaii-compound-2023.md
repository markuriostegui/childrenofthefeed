# Inside Mark Zuckerberg’s Top-Secret Hawaii Compound

## Factual Summary

- This December 14, 2023 WIRED investigation reports that Zuckerberg's Kauai compound includes a 5,000-square-foot underground shelter connected by tunnel to the main mansions.
- The article says the shelter includes living space, a mechanical room, and an escape hatch, and that its door would be metal-filled concrete in a bunker style.
- It also reports strict NDAs, separation between work crews, heavy surveillance, self-sufficiency features, and food and water arrangements that go well beyond an ordinary vacation home.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- WIRED writes that different construction crews were "forbidden from speaking with other crews about their work."
- It reports that the compound "will ... include a 5,000-square-foot underground shelter, have its own energy and food supplies."
- The tunnel branches into the shelter, which features "living space, a mechanical room, and an escape hatch."
- The article says the shelter door is a style "common in bunkers and bomb shelters."
