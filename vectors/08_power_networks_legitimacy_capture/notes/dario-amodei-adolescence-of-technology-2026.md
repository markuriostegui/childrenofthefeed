# The Adolescence of Technology

## Factual Summary

- This January 1, 2026 essay by Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei lays out his own framework for AI risk, growth, backlash, job displacement, philanthropy, and wealth concentration.
- It is a primary source for how a frontier-lab founder describes the social consequences he believes may accompany AI progress, including public backlash and destabilizing concentration of wealth.
- The essay also states that Anthropic's co-founders have pledged to donate 80% of their wealth and that staff have pledged company shares worth billions at current prices, with company matching commitments.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- Amodei writes that "a public backlash against AI is brewing."
- He says "All of Anthropic's co-founders have pledged to donate 80% of our wealth."
- He warns that "The thing to worry about is a level of wealth concentration that will break society."
- He argues that if economic leverage disappears for ordinary citizens, "the implicit social contract of democracy may stop working."
