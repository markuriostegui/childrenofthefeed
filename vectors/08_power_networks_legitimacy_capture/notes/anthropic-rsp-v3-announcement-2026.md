# Responsible Scaling Policy Version 3.0

## Factual Summary

- Anthropic's announcement page for RSP v3.0 publicly explains the company's revised safety framework and why it changed.
- The announcement emphasizes industry-wide safety recommendations, frontier safety roadmaps, risk reports, and governance commitments.
- It frames the revision as a response to competitive collective-action problems rather than as a declaration that catastrophic risk no longer matters.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- The revised policy distinguishes Anthropic's own plans from broader industry-wide recommendations.
- The announcement ties the framework to risk reports, governance, and public-facing safety roadmaps.
- It presents the shift as a response to a collective-action problem among frontier developers.
