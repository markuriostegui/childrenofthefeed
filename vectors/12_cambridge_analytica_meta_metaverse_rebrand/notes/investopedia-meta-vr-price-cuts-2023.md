# Meta Slashes Prices for VR Headsets

## Factual Summary

- Investopedia reported in March 2023 that Meta cut prices for Quest headsets while facing weak VR demand and heavy losses in Reality Labs.
- The article ties the cuts to declining shipments, large 2022 losses, and investor pressure to reduce spending on the metaverse.
