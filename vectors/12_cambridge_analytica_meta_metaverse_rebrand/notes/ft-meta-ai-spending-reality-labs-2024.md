# Meta sees AI spending accelerating as earnings top forecasts

## Factual Summary

- On October 30, 2024, the FT reported that Meta's earnings topped forecasts while investor reaction remained muted because spending was accelerating and Reality Labs continued posting large losses.
- The piece described Zuckerberg as pivoting Meta toward AI after investors had become alarmed by the earlier lossmaking metaverse bet.
- The report treated AI momentum and metaverse losses as simultaneous parts of the company's financial story, not as unrelated episodes.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- The FT said investor response was muted even after strong results because Meta expected significant spending growth and posted big Reality Labs losses.
- The article said Zuckerberg had begun pivoting the company from his avatar-filled metaverse bet after investor alarm.
- The report tied AI enthusiasm to a still-unresolved burden from Reality Labs.
