# With Reality Labs, Meta risks heading down slippery slope of overspending again

## Factual Summary

- MarketWatch reported in late 2024 that Meta's Reality Labs losses were again raising fears of overspending despite the company's broader market recovery.
- The piece records continued multibillion-dollar Reality Labs losses and ongoing investor concern over whether Meta was repeating an expensive strategic pattern.
- It is useful as a financial-pressure source for the metaverse cycle, not as a source on motive.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- Reality Labs losses were described as a renewed source of investor wariness.
- The article lists losses of $13.7 billion in 2022, $16.1 billion in 2023, and $12.8 billion in the first nine months of 2024.
- Investor skepticism was tied to fears of slipping back into overspending.
