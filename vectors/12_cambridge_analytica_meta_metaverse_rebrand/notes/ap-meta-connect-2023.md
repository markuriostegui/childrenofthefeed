# Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg kicks off developer conference with focus on AI, virtual reality

## Factual Summary

- On September 27, 2023, AP reported that Meta used its Connect developer conference to keep selling a metaverse-centered long-term story while also foregrounding AI as a product and engagement engine.
- The report says Meta unveiled Quest 3, promoted smart glasses and AI assistants, and repeated Zuckerberg's language about the metaverse as the future of human connection.
- AP also framed the transition as slower and more expensive than expected, stressing that Meta's core business still remained advertising on Facebook and Instagram.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- Zuckerberg said the physical and digital would come together in what Meta calls the metaverse.
- AP wrote that Meta's transformation was slower than expected and had already cost billions of dollars.
- The piece said Meta's main business remained advertising and that competition with TikTok was still the company's biggest challenge.
- AP noted that Meta had cut more than 20,000 jobs while focusing on technical hiring tied to AI and the company's long-term vision.

## What It Proves

- That even two years after the rebrand, Meta was still publicly tying its corporate identity to the metaverse.
- That the metaverse pitch coexisted with a newer AI-centered engagement and monetization strategy rather than disappearing cleanly.
- That serious reporting could already describe the transformation as slower than expected, costly, and still subordinate to the ad business.

## What It Suggests

- That the post-rebrand period can be read as a prolonged strategic transition in which immersive-world rhetoric and AI rhetoric overlapped.
- That Meta's public story was shifting, but not by admitting failure or formally abandoning the metaverse frame.

## What It Does Not Prove

- It does not prove that the rebrand itself was undertaken to distract from a specific regulatory event.
- It does not prove that Meta had internally abandoned the metaverse by September 2023.
- It does not prove monocausally why investors, users, or management treated the strategy as underperforming.
