# COVID as accelerator and geopolitical arena

## ID

`03_covid_acceleration_and_geopolitics`

## Target Chapters

- `03`

## Working Thesis

COVID accelerated digital dependency, platform concentration, and mediated governance while leaving origin claims officially unresolved.

## Guiding Questions

- What is firmly documented?
- What is disputed?
- Which connections should be treated as hypotheses?

## Exclusions

- Do not turn conjectures into facts.
- Do not use excluded outlets as factual support.
- Do not lose date and source traceability.

## Key Disputes

- Whether the strongest documentary reading is acceleration and concentration rather than intentional geopolitical design.
- How far origin uncertainty can sustain geopolitical interpretation without slipping into unsupported total-intent claims.
- How to distinguish documented effects on work, screens, surveillance, and platform power from wider Cold War analogies.
