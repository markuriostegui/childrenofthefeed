# Queue

## High Priority

- Official and serious sources on COVID origins uncertainty, lockdown effects, and platform consolidation.
- Economic and institutional reporting on remote work, digital dependency, and accelerated mediation.
- Material that clearly separates proven effects from geopolitical interpretation.

## Medium Priority

- Serious think-tank or policy analysis on strategic consequences of the pandemic.
- Corroborated long-form interviews or hearings.

## Low Priority

- Speculative material that cannot clear the evidence threshold.
