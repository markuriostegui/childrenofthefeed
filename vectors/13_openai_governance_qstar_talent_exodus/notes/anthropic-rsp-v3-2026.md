# Anthropic’s Responsible Scaling Policy (version 3.0)

## Factual Summary

- Anthropic's RSP v3.0 took effect on February 24, 2026 and describes itself as a voluntary framework for managing catastrophic risks from advanced AI systems.
- The document explicitly says the company changed its approach because catastrophic risk depends on the actions of multiple developers, not one firm acting alone.
- It separates Anthropic's own plans from more ambitious industry-wide recommendations and ties those recommendations to advocacy, governance, and competitor-contingent commitments.

- The RSP is a "voluntary framework for managing catastrophic risks from advanced AI systems."
