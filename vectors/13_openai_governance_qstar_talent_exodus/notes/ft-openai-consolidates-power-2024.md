# Who's left at OpenAI? Sam Altman consolidates power after failed coup

## Factual Summary

- On September 28, 2024, the FT reported that Sam Altman had consolidated power at OpenAI after the failed 2023 coup and that a new wave of departures showed the company had not fully healed from that rupture.
- The report highlighted Mira Murati's exit together with departures by Bob McGrew and Barret Zoph, and framed the company as moving from a scrappy research organization toward a commercial giant.
- The article treated the exits as evidence of strategic reorientation as well as organizational fracture.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- The FT said Murati's exit shocked staff and made clear that OpenAI had not healed from the fractures caused by the failed autumn coup.
- The article reported that several high-profile departures came as OpenAI pursued a more commercially oriented direction.
- The FT framed the post-crisis structure as one in which Altman had consolidated authority.
