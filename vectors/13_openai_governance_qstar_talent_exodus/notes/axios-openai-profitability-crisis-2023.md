# How tensions over profitability at OpenAI turned into a full-blown crisis

## Factual Summary

- This November 20, 2023 Axios analysis frames the Altman crisis as a collision between OpenAI's nonprofit mission structure and the commercial imperatives of its for-profit subsidiary.
- The piece identifies four internal power centers: the board, senior management, employees, and outside investors led by Microsoft.
- Used carefully, it helps move vector 13 from a single-event narrative toward a broader governance pattern around mission, capital, productization, and control.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- Axios says tensions between the nonprofit and for-profit sides "erupted" into a governance crisis.
- The article states that the for-profit side provided privileged access for investors like Microsoft.
- It says OpenAI had four separate power centers whose interests were not fully aligned.
- Axios also notes earlier employee concern that OpenAI was becoming too commercial under Altman and Brockman.

## What It Proves

- That a serious, mainstream account of the crisis centered on structural tension between mission governance and commercial expansion.

## What It Suggests
