# OpenAI co-founder Ilya Sutskever to exit firm

## Factual Summary

- Axios reported on May 14, 2024 that OpenAI cofounder and chief scientist Ilya Sutskever was leaving the company after months of uncertainty following the November 2023 board crisis.
- The article includes both Altman's public statement and Sutskever's own confirmation, making it a useful bridge between governance rupture and talent reconfiguration.
- For vector 13, the note matters less as scandal material than as evidence that the crisis had durable consequences for top technical leadership.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- Axios says Sutskever's future had been in doubt for months after he participated in the board move against Altman and then reversed course.
- Altman said publicly that Sutskever and OpenAI were parting ways.
- Sutskever confirmed his departure and said he remained confident OpenAI would build AGI that is safe and beneficial.
- Axios reports that Jakub Pachocki would be promoted to chief scientist.

## What It Proves

- That one of OpenAI's most important technical founders exited in the aftermath of the governance crisis.
- That the board conflict was not a closed one-week episode; it reshaped leadership over the following months.
- That succession in frontier labs can involve both governance rhetoric and changes in technical command.

## What It Suggests

- That capability leadership, safety positioning, and internal control were tightly linked in OpenAI's post-crisis reorganization.
- That later departures should be read as part of a pattern rather than as isolated resignations.

## What It Does Not Prove

- It does not prove why Sutskever chose to leave beyond the public record.
- It does not prove disagreement over any specific model or secret research program.
- It does not prove that every later departure stemmed from the same cause.
