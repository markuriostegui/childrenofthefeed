# OpenAI board forms safety and security committee

## Factual Summary

- On May 28, 2024, OpenAI announced that its board had formed a Safety and Security Committee to make recommendations on critical safety and security decisions across OpenAI projects.
- The statement named Bret Taylor, Adam D'Angelo, Nicole Seligman, and Sam Altman as committee members, and later noted that Paul Nakasone joined the committee upon his appointment to the board.
- OpenAI said it had recently begun training its next frontier model and framed the committee as part of a 90-day review of safeguards, with a promise of a later public update.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- OpenAI said the committee would make recommendations to the full board on critical safety and security decisions.
- The company said it had recently begun training its next frontier model and anticipated a next level of capabilities on its path to AGI.
- The statement named John Schulman, Jakub Pachocki, Aleksander Madry, Lilian Weng, Matt Knight, Rob Joyce, and John Carlin as supporting technical or external figures.
