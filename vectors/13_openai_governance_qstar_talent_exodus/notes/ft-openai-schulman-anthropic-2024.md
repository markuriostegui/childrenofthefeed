# OpenAI co-founder departs for rival Anthropic

## Factual Summary

- On August 6, 2024, the FT reported that OpenAI co-founder John Schulman left to join Anthropic, presenting the move as the latest in a succession of senior departures from OpenAI.
- The report said Schulman framed the move around AI alignment and a return to more hands-on technical work, while also noting Greg Brockman's leave and earlier departures including Ilya Sutskever.
- The article placed the move in the longer shadow of the November 2023 board crisis and ongoing disputes about OpenAI's pace and direction.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- The FT said Schulman left for Anthropic to focus more deeply on AI alignment.
- The report described the departure as part of a succession of senior exits from OpenAI in recent months.
- The article connected those exits to criticism of OpenAI's pace and direction after the boardroom crisis.
