# OpenAI Chief Technology Officer Mira Murati and 2 other execs are leaving the ChatGPT maker

## Factual Summary

- AP reported on September 25, 2024 that Mira Murati was leaving OpenAI and that chief research officer Bob McGrew and research leader Barret Zoph were also departing.
- The article places Murati's exit inside a continuing pattern of executive turnover after the 2023 board crisis and subsequent departures of Sutskever, Schulman, and Brockman.
- Sam Altman publicly framed the departures as independent and amicable, while AP emphasized their cumulative governance significance.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- Murati said she was leaving to create time and space for her own exploration.
- AP linked the exits to a broader leadership exodus at OpenAI.
- Altman said leadership transitions are expected in companies growing so quickly.

## What It Proves

- That the OpenAI exodus broadened beyond one or two figures and continued into late 2024.
- That Murati, McGrew, and Zoph departures turned the story from a single founder rupture into a deeper leadership reconfiguration.
- That Chapter 6 and Chapter 8 can discuss executive churn as an ongoing governance pattern rather than a one-week board scandal.

## What It Suggests

- That OpenAI's post-crisis stabilization remained incomplete even after the board reset.
- That frontier-model commercialization, safety posture, and internal authority likely remained entangled in the background.

## What It Does Not Prove

- It does not prove a single hidden cause for all departures.
- It does not prove Murati, McGrew, or Zoph left because of the same disagreement.
- It does not by itself prove capability anxiety or Q* drove the exits.
