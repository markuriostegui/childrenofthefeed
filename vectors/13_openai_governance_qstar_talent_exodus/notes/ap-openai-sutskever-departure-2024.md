# OpenAI co-founder Ilya Sutskever announces departure from ChatGPT maker

## Factual Summary

- On May 15, 2024, AP reported that OpenAI co-founder and chief scientist Ilya Sutskever was leaving the company after nearly a decade.
- AP linked the departure directly to the November 2023 board crisis by noting that Sutskever helped remove Altman and was the executive who told him he was being fired.
- The piece also documented succession, naming Jakub Pachocki as chief scientist, and recorded Altman's public praise for both Sutskever and Pachocki.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- AP said Sutskever was one of four board members who voted to push out Altman and then quickly reinstate him.
- Sutskever said he was moving on to a new project that was very personally meaningful but offered no further details.
- AP reported that Sutskever would be replaced by Jakub Pachocki as chief scientist.

## What It Proves

- That one of OpenAI's core technical founders exited within months of the governance crisis.
- That the November 2023 rupture continued to reshape technical leadership well after Altman's return.
- That the company's public message paired succession with mission language about safe progress toward AGI.

## What It Suggests

- That talent exits at OpenAI can be treated as part of a longer governance pattern rather than a sealed one-week scandal.
- That leadership change in frontier labs often bundles personnel, mission rhetoric, and public reassurance together.

## What It Does Not Prove

- It does not prove why Sutskever chose to leave beyond the limited explanation he gave publicly.
- It does not prove that the exit was caused solely by the board crisis.
- It does not prove a unified internal factional story about safety, capability, or commercialization.
