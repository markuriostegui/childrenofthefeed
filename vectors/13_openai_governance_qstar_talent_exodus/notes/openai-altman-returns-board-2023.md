# Sam Altman returns as CEO, OpenAI has a new initial board

## Factual Summary

- On November 29, 2023, OpenAI announced Sam Altman's return as CEO together with a reset of the board and a formal governance-review process.
- The statement says the new initial board would consist of Bret Taylor, Larry Summers, and Adam D'Angelo, and that Microsoft would receive a non-voting observer seat.
- This source is central for documenting how OpenAI stabilized the crisis institutionally: by combining restored executive leadership, board reconfiguration, safety language, and an explicit governance overhaul.

## Key Quotes or Findings

- OpenAI says Altman returned as CEO and Mira Murati returned as CTO.
- The company says the new initial board would consist of Bret Taylor, Larry Summers, and Adam D'Angelo.
- Altman says one immediate priority is to further invest in full-stack safety efforts.
- Bret Taylor says the board will build a qualified and diverse board, oversee an independent review, and strengthen OpenAI's governance structure, while Microsoft will have a non-voting observer.
