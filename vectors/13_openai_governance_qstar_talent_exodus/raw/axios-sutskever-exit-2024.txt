Sam Altman and Ilya Sutskever. Photo: Jack Guez / AFP via Getty Images
After months
in limbo
, OpenAI chief scientist Ilya Sutskever is officially leaving the company he co-founded.
What they're saying:
"Ilya and OpenAI are going to part ways," CEO Sam Altman said in a
post
on X (formerly Twitter).
"This is very sad to me; Ilya is easily one of the greatest minds of our generation, a guiding light of our field, and a dear friend," Altman added. "His brilliance and vision are well known; his warmth and compassion are less well known but no less important.
Sutskever
confirmed his departure
on X writing, "After almost a decade, I have made the decision to leave OpenAI.  The company's trajectory has been nothing short of miraculous, and I'm confident that OpenAI will build AGI that is both safe and beneficial."
"I am excited for what comes next — a project that is very personally meaningful to me about which I will share details in due time," Sutskever added.
Between the lines
: Sutskever's future with the company has been in doubt since, while on the board, he voted to
oust Altman
from his role as CEO, only to reverse course and sign an open letter demanding his return.
What's next:
Jakub Pachocki
, the company's director of research, is being promoted to chief scientist, according to
the company's blog post
.
