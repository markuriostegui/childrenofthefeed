Relevant extracted text:
- Anthropic describes the RSP as a voluntary framework for managing catastrophic risks from advanced AI systems.
- The policy says its third iteration changed because the overall level of catastrophic risk depends on the actions of multiple AI developers, not just one.
- The document says Anthropic can no longer commit to following ambitious industry-wide safety recommendations unilaterally.
- It links the policy to advocacy through industry groups and policy advocacy.
- The framework includes governance commitments, external review, risk reports, and competitor-contingent commitments.
