Doctrine

AI IS THE PATRIMONY OF HUMANITY

This publication series exists to empower humanity and place the ownership question in plain view from the first screen.

This research program argues that patrimony is the civilizational core, Section 230 reform answers the platform-extraction stage, and anti-capture obligations answer the state-corporate enclosure stage.

  1. Frontier AI was trained on humanity's collective archive.
  2. That archive cannot default into exclusive private enclosure.
  3. Public-trust governance, broad access, and anti-monopoly limits must follow.

Foundational public essay: AI Copyright Weights: A New Frontier in Intellectual Property Law

Published and advanced by WakenAI Labs.

How to read a paper

Use the footnotes to open real external sources. Use the paper-local appendices to understand why each source matters in that paper. Use the corpus companion when a paper mentions a registry code such as Source S-066 or Claim C-212.
