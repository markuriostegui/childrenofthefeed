## Historical or Institutional Context

The U.S. Copyright Office has already treated AI training on copyrighted works as a major live policy problem rather than a settled technical inevitability. The 2023 inquiry and 2025 report together show that training-input legality, disclosure, and compensation were contested near the start of the generative-AI boom and remain contested now. ^[Sources: GovInfo, [Copyright and Artificial Intelligence...](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2023-08-30/pdf/2023-18624.pdf) (2023-08-30; see [Appendix A4](#appendix-a4-copyright-and-artificial-intelligence-notice-of-inquiry)); U.S. Copyright Office, [Copyright and Artificial Intelligence...](https://copyright.gov/ai/Copyright-and-Artificial-Intelligence-Part-3-Generative-AI-Training-Report-Pre-Publication-Version.pdf) (2025-05-09; see [Appendix A1](#appendix-a1-copyright-and-artificial-intelligence-part-3-generative-ai-training-pre-publication-version))]

## Main Analysis

The technical and legal record also undermines the industry's preferred abstraction. Research on memorization shows that large language models can reproduce identifiable material from training data, while court records and legal scholarship now separate at least two layers of conflict: training-use doctrine and input-acquisition legality. ^[Sources: arXiv, [Extracting Training Data from Large L...](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.07805) (2020-12-14; see [Appendix A5](#appendix-a5-extracting-training-data-from-large-language-models)); arXiv, [Generative AI Training and Copyright Law](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.15858) (2025-02-21; see [Appendix A6](#appendix-a6-generative-ai-training-and-copyright-law)); U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California via CourtListener, [Bartz et al. v. The social-to-model bridge is also explicit. GPT-2's WebText was built from socially filtered outbound links shared on Reddit, and by February 2024 Reddit had moved from diffuse public conversation to direct licensing, striking a major agreement that allowed Google to use Reddit posts for AI training and related product improvement. The extraction economy did not end with unpaid capture; it evolved into direct licensing over accumulated human expression. ^[Sources: OpenAI, [Language Models are Unsupervised Mult...](https://cdn.openai.com/better-language-models/language_models_are_unsupervised_multitask_learners.pdf) (2019-02-14; see [Appendix A2](#appendix-a2-language-models-are-unsupervised-multitask-learners)); AP, [Reddit strikes $60M deal allowing Goo...](https://apnews.com/article/a7f131c7cb4225307134ef21d3c6a708) (2024-02-22; see [Appendix A3](#appendix-a3-reddit-strikes-60m-deal-allowing-google-to-train-ai-models-on-its-posts-unveils-ipo-plans))]

The labor layer matters too. Research on annotation and outsourced human labor shows that model building depends not only on scraped creative works but also on significant human verification, correction, and operational support that is often geographically externalized and weakly valued. ^[Sources: Big Data & Society, [The problem with annotation. Human la...](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/20539517231188723) (2023-07-25; see [Appendix A9](#appendix-a9-the-problem-with-annotation-human-labour-and-outsourcing-between-france-and-madagascar))]

Taken together, the record shows a sequence in which collectively produced expression becomes training input, archived conversation becomes licensable model fuel, and additional human labor is used to clean, annotate, and operationalize the resulting systems. Value that first appeared as diffuse public production is progressively reorganized inside proprietary model pipelines. ^[Sources: OpenAI, [Language Models are Unsupervised Mult...](https://cdn.openai.com/better-language-models/language_models_are_unsupervised_multitask_learners.pdf) (2019-02-14; see [Appendix A2](#appendix-a2-language-models-are-unsupervised-multitask-learners)); AP, [Reddit strikes $60M deal allowing Goo...](https://apnews.com/article/a7f131c7cb4225307134ef21d3c6a708) (2024-02-22; see [Appendix A3](#appendix-a3-reddit-strikes-60m-deal-allowing-google-to-train-ai-models-on-its-posts-unveils-ipo-plans)); Big Data & Society, [The problem with annotation. Human la...](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/20539517231188723) (2023-07-25; see [Appendix A9](#appendix-a9-the-problem-with-annotation-human-labour-and-outsourcing-between-france-and-madagascar))]

Interpretation: the strategic move is not only model creation but control of the interface through which machine-packaged intelligence is accessed. In that reading, the shelf is the interface: the prompt box, chatbot, or subscription layer becomes the first point of contact between a buyer and capability distilled from human work. Once that first contact is captured, value can be repriced downward and dependence can deepen even where total technical replacement remains incomplete. ^[Sources: OpenAI, [Language Models are Unsupervised Mult...](https://cdn.openai.com/better-language-models/language_models_are_unsupervised_multitask_learners.pdf) (2019-02-14; see [Appendix A2](#appendix-a2-language-models-are-unsupervised-multitask-learners)); AP, [Reddit strikes $60M deal allowing Goo...](https://apnews.com/article/a7f131c7cb4225307134ef21d3c6a708) (2024-02-22; see [Appendix A3](#appendix-a3-reddit-strikes-60m-deal-allowing-google-to-train-ai-models-on-its-posts-unveils-ipo-plans)); Big Data & Society, [The problem with annotation. Human la...](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/20539517231188723) (2023-07-25; see [Appendix A9](#appendix-a9-the-problem-with-annotation-human-labour-and-outsourcing-between-france-and-madagascar))]

The House AI Task Force report gives this chapter an institutional bridge beyond agencies and courts. By December 17, 2024, a bipartisan congressional report was already recording the training conflict in language overlapping with the dossier's core concerns about commercialization, consent, credit, compensation, and derivative-work theory. ^[Sources: U.S. House of Representatives, [Bipartisan House Task Force Report on...](https://www.speaker.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/AI-Task-Force-Report-FINAL.pdf) (2024-12-17; see [Appendix A10](#appendix-a10-bipartisan-house-task-force-report-on-artificial-intelligence)); TwinChat, [AI Copyright Weights: A New Frontier...](https://medium.com/twinchat/ai-copyright-weights-a-new-frontier-in-intellectual-property-law-d8ee1b6c55ee) (n.d.; see [Appendix A11](#appendix-a11-ai-copyright-weights-a-new-frontier-in-intellectual-property-law))]

Interpretation: this is the dossier's primary evidentiary base for `AI Weights as Patrimony of Humanity`. If frontier weights are built from collectively produced human expression and labor, then they cannot be treated as ordinary private assets by default. The strongest normative direction pushes toward broad, open, utility-like availability under public-interest rules rather than permanent private scarcity. That is advocated doctrine, not settled law. ^[Sources: TwinChat, [AI Copyright Weights: A New Frontier...](https://medium.com/twinchat/ai-copyright-weights-a-new-frontier-in-intellectual-property-law-d8ee1b6c55ee) (n.d.; see [Appendix A11](#appendix-a11-ai-copyright-weights-a-new-frontier-in-intellectual-property-law)); U.S. Copyright Office, [Copyright and Artificial Intelligence...](https://copyright.gov/ai/Copyright-and-Artificial-Intelligence-Part-3-Generative-AI-Training-Report-Pre-Publication-Version.pdf) (2025-05-09; see [Appendix A1](#appendix-a1-copyright-and-artificial-intelligence-part-3-generative-ai-training-pre-publication-version)); U.S. House of Representatives, [Bipartisan House Task Force Report on...](https://www.speaker.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/AI-Task-Force-Report-FINAL.pdf) (2024-12-17; see [Appendix A10](#appendix-a10-bipartisan-house-task-force-report-on-artificial-intelligence))]

## Reform Relevance

Once captured human production can be recombined into proprietary weights, the surveillance economy acquires a new layer. It no longer only predicts behavior. It begins to substitute for the producers whose accumulated traces made the system possible in the first place. That is why the ownership question has to expand into a governance question. ^[Sources: OpenAI, [Language Models are Unsupervised Mult...](https://cdn.openai.com/better-language-models/language_models_are_unsupervised_multitask_learners.pdf) (2019-02-14; see [Appendix A2](#appendix-a2-language-models-are-unsupervised-multitask-learners)); AP, [Reddit strikes $60M deal allowing Goo...](https://apnews.com/article/a7f131c7cb4225307134ef21d3c6a708) (2024-02-22; see [Appendix A3](#appendix-a3-reddit-strikes-60m-deal-allowing-google-to-train-ai-models-on-its-posts-unveils-ipo-plans)); TwinChat, [AI Copyright Weights: A New Frontier...](https://medium.com/twinchat/ai-copyright-weights-a-new-frontier-in-intellectual-property-law-d8ee1b6c55ee) (n.d.; see [Appendix A11](#appendix-a11-ai-copyright-weights-a-new-frontier-in-intellectual-property-law))]
