## Main Analysis

The Instagram body-image and TikTok under-13 records show that harms are not confined to public feeds. Private-message dynamics, recommendation mismatches, and child-directed product failures all matter. Platform design is therefore part of the harm structure, not just the venue in which the harm appears. ^[Sources: arXiv, [Unfiltered: How Teens Engage in Body...](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.02176) (2025-04-02; see [Appendix A4](#appendix-a4-unfiltered-how-teens-engage-in-body-image-and-shaming-discussions-via-instagram-direct-messages-dms)); arXiv, [When Kids Mode Isn't For Kids: Invest...](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.00299) (2025-06-30; see [Appendix A5](#appendix-a5-when-kids-mode-isn-t-for-kids-investigating-tiktok-s-under-13-experience))]

The trust and polarization literature strengthens the civic layer. Social media trust, content trust, and user trust are analytically distinct, but all are reshaped by platform architecture. Cross-national Pew reporting adds that large majorities across surveyed countries associate social media with manipulation, division, and reduced civility, while the United States appears as an unusually negative outlier. ^[Sources: arXiv, [What Do We Mean When We Talk about Tr...](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.03671) (2023-02-07; see [Appendix A2](#appendix-a2-what-do-we-mean-when-we-talk-about-trust-in-social-media-a-systematic-review)); arXiv, [Quantifying social organization and p...](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.00590) (2020-10-01; see [Appendix A3](#appendix-a3-quantifying-social-organization-and-political-polarization-in-online-platforms)); Pew Research Center, [Social Media Seen as Mostly Good for...](https://www.pewresearch.org/global/2022/12/06/social-media-seen-as-mostly-good-for-democracy-across-many-nations-but-u-s-is-a-major-outlier/) (2022-12-06; see [Appendix A6](#appendix-a6-social-media-seen-as-mostly-good-for-democracy-across-many-nations-but-u-s-is-a-major-outlier))]

The dossier's more morally charged cultural claims also have bounded support. Serious reporting documents recommendation systems intensifying misogynistic content exposure. Research on OnlyFans documents mainstream entry by creators with no prior sex-work history and ties that normalization to platform visibility and pandemic-era adoption. These examples should remain illustrative, but they are not empty rhetoric. ^[Sources: The Guardian, [Social media algorithms 'amplifying m...](https://www.theguardian.com/media/2024/feb/06/social-media-algorithms-amplifying-misogynistic-content) (2024-02-06; see [Appendix A7](#appendix-a7-social-media-algorithms-amplifying-misogynistic-content)); arXiv, [Nudes? Shouldn't I charge for these?:...](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.10425) (2022-05-20; see [Appendix A8](#appendix-a8-nudes-shouldn-t-i-charge-for-these-motivations-of-new-sexual-content-creators-on-onlyfans))]

Interpretation: Chapter `02` deepens the case for `Refactor Section 230` because the social harms here are not reducible to isolated user speech. They emerge from recommendation systems, exposure design, monetized aspiration, and systemic amplification. A sovereign-scale legal framework should therefore focus on design responsibility and measurable harm, not only on moderation after the fact. ^[Sources: HHS, [Social Media and Youth Mental Health:...](https://www.hhs.gov/surgeongeneral/priorities/youth-mental-health/social-media/index.html) (n.d.; see [Appendix A1](#appendix-a1-social-media-and-youth-mental-health-the-u-s-surgeon-general-s-advisory)); arXiv, [When Kids Mode Isn't For Kids: Invest...](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.00299) (2025-06-30; see [Appendix A5](#appendix-a5-when-kids-mode-isn-t-for-kids-investigating-tiktok-s-under-13-experience)); Pew Research Center, [Social Media Seen as Mostly Good for...](https://www.pewresearch.org/global/2022/12/06/social-media-seen-as-mostly-good-for-democracy-across-many-nations-but-u-s-is-a-major-outlier/) (2022-12-06; see [Appendix A6](#appendix-a6-social-media-seen-as-mostly-good-for-democracy-across-many-nations-but-u-s-is-a-major-outlier))]

## Reform Relevance

If attention is trained by systems optimized for engagement rather than flourishing, then the outcome is not only distraction. It is a weaker public with thinner trust, more manipulable norms, and reduced civic resilience. That matters because a society softened in this way is easier to govern through later layers of digital mediation and AI dependency. ^[Sources: arXiv, [What Do We Mean When We Talk about Tr...](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.03671) (2023-02-07; see [Appendix A2](#appendix-a2-what-do-we-mean-when-we-talk-about-trust-in-social-media-a-systematic-review)); arXiv, [Quantifying social organization and p...](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.00590) (2020-10-01; see [Appendix A3](#appendix-a3-quantifying-social-organization-and-political-polarization-in-online-platforms)); Pew Research Center, [Social Media Seen as Mostly Good for...](https://www.pewresearch.org/global/2022/12/06/social-media-seen-as-mostly-good-for-democracy-across-many-nations-but-u-s-is-a-major-outlier/) (2022-12-06; see [Appendix A6](#appendix-a6-social-media-seen-as-mostly-good-for-democracy-across-many-nations-but-u-s-is-a-major-outlier))]

## Conclusion

If attention is trained by systems optimized for engagement rather than flourishing, then the outcome is not only distraction. The next historical shock did not create this architecture.
