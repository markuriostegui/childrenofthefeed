## Reform Relevance

The dossier therefore does not end in a vague call for better ethics. It moves toward three explicit reforms:

- `Refactor Section 230`
- `AI Weights as Patrimony of Humanity`
- `Limit Government Capture by AI Companies`

Interpretation: those reforms are not presented as already enacted or legally settled. They are the dossier's normative answer to the documented structure of extraction, enclosure, and capture. ^[Sources: TwinChat, [AI Copyright Weights: A New Frontier...](https://medium.com/twinchat/ai-copyright-weights-a-new-frontier-in-intellectual-property-law-d8ee1b6c55ee) (n.d.; see [Appendix A1](#appendix-a1-ai-copyright-weights-a-new-frontier-in-intellectual-property-law)); TwinChat, [How AI Is Ruining Your Life to Build...](https://medium.com/twinchat/how-ai-is-ruining-your-life-to-build-an-empire-8b6d02e55bc9) (n.d.; see [Appendix A2](#appendix-a2-how-ai-is-ruining-your-life-to-build-an-empire)); U.S. House of Representatives, [Bipartisan House Task Force Report on...](https://www.speaker.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/AI-Task-Force-Report-FINAL.pdf) (2024-12-17; see [Appendix A3](#appendix-a3-bipartisan-house-task-force-report-on-artificial-intelligence))]
