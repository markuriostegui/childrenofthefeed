\subsection{Abstract}\label{abstract}

Frontier firms do not consolidate power by product alone. OpenAI
publicly treated the November 2023 crisis as a governance rupture and
responded with a visible legitimacy-repair process that included board
reset, formal review, and later restructuring. Once legitimacy is
institutionally assembled, frontier AI stops looking like just another
industry and begins to look like a state-adjacent governing
infrastructure.
