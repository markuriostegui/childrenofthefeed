\subsection{Abstract}\label{abstract}

The response program is no longer an open-ended menu. The platform
chapters already established that sovereign-scale systems shape
exposure, amplification, targeting, monetization, and moderation
conditions at mass scale. The essential political choice is whether
intelligence infrastructure will remain a privately concentrated empire
built on captured social life, or whether societies will recover
sovereignty over the terms on which cognition itself is organized.

duties commensurate with their power. \footnote{Sources: FTC,
