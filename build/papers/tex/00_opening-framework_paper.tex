products, model benchmarks, or corporate market share. By December 17,
2024, the Bipartisan House Task Force on Artificial Intelligence had
already recorded the training-data conflict in terms overlapping with
the dossier's own framework: reproduction, derivative-work theory,
commercialization, consent, credit, and compensation. The dossier
therefore does not end in a vague call for better ethics.

\subsection{Scope and Framing Note}\label{scope-and-framing-note}

This paper examines opening framework within the broader dossier
\emph{From Surveillance Capitalism to AI Imperialism}. It is
intentionally written to circulate on its own: necessary context is
restated, evidence boundaries are made explicit, and cited material is
reproduced in paper-local form rather than delegated to repo navigation.
It contributes to the series as a paper that frames the civilizational
ownership question and announces the three-reform destination. Adjacent
reinforcement appears in Chapter 01, Chapter 04, Chapter 10, but those
cross-references are supportive rather than required for basic
comprehension.

\subsection{Central Thesis}\label{central-thesis}

The fight over artificial intelligence is not only a fight over
products, model benchmarks, or corporate market share. It is also a
fight over who owns the condensed value of collective human cognition.
In this dossier, the central question is whether captured human
language, art, code, labor, culture, science, and emotion can be turned
into privately governed strategic weights without triggering a deeper
political challenge. \footnote{Sources: TwinChat,

\subsection{Main Analysis}\label{main-analysis}

Paul Lara's published writing adds the dossier's second framing line:
technofeudalism, debt-heavy buildout, paper valuation, and the
smoke-economy vocabulary needed to describe how extraction is financed
and politically sold. The dossier uses that line as interpretive
framing, not as a substitute for primary evidence. \footnote{Sources:
  Paul Lara,
  \href{https://www.dineroenimagen.com/paul-lara/como-hackear-al-tecnofeudalismo/186955}{¿Cómo

Interpretation: the strongest defensible reading is not that every later
chapter proves a single grand conspiracy. It is that a coherent line can
be drawn from platform extraction, to social weakening, to pandemic
acceleration, to model training, to labor correction, to access

dossier is to keep the factual layer and the political interpretation
distinct while still arguing forcefully from the evidence. \footnote{Sources:

\subsection{Reform Relevance}\label{reform-relevance}

The dossier therefore does not end in a vague call for better ethics. It
moves toward three explicit reforms:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \texttt{Refactor\ Section\ 230}
\item
  \texttt{AI\ Weights\ as\ Patrimony\ of\ Humanity}
\item
  \texttt{Limit\ Government\ Capture\ by\ AI\ Companies}
\end{itemize}

Interpretation: those reforms are not presented as already enacted or
legally settled. They are the dossier's normative answer to the
documented structure of extraction, enclosure, and capture. \footnote{Sources:

The dossier therefore does not end in a vague call for better ethics. To

the dossier's three reforms.
